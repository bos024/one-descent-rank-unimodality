\section{A two-seed LC closure target for the sharpened entrance}
This is a new OPEN structural bridge. The exact identification and
the two seed bounds below are proved; the proposed closure is NOT.
It is not registered as a general C or original UI result.

Let $L=\prod_{t\in V}[t]$ have degree $h=\sum(t-1)$ and let
$m=u-1$. Write $F_{2^m}=\sum_{k=0}^m(z+z^2)^k$, and define the
SIGNED polynomial
\[
 C_{m,\alpha}(z)=L(z)\{F_{2^m}(z)
                   -z^{m+\alpha}(1+z)^{m+1}\}.         \tag{1}
\]
The infinite-padding case omits the subtracted term. Put
$A_n=\max([z^n]C_{m,\alpha},0)$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact tail and strictly positive first two entrance minors]
For the ACTUAL arm $1^\alpha2^uV$, with $r=|V|$, $W=h+r$,
\[
            \Delta F_{1^\alpha2^uV}(r+2+n)
                           =[z^n]C_{m,\alpha}\quad(n\ge h-1). \tag{2}
\]
At $u=h+1$ (so $m=h$), the positive-part array in (1) has strictly
positive LC minors at BOTH centres $n=m$ and $n=m+1$, for EVERY
padding $\alpha\ge1$, with no tail-count or capacity bound.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The tail expansion gives $F=Z_V+z^r L F_{1^\alpha2^u}$, where
$\deg Z_V\le W-t_1\le W-3$. The exact binary signed derivative is
\[
 (1-z)F_{1^\alpha2^u}=1+z^2F_{2^{u-1}}
                         -z^{u+\alpha+1}(1+z)^u.
\]
After multiplication by $z^rL$, the extra term $z^rL$ has degree
$W$. The derivative of $Z_V$ has degree at most $W-2$.
At physical ranks $r+2+n\ge W+1$ both vanish, proving (2).

At the entrance let $g=u+r=W+1$, $N_0=g+2=W+3$, and denote
the reference slopes at $W+1,W+2,W+3,W+4$ by $b,c,d,e$.
Let $A>0$ be the entire-word Cassini amplitude.
Then $d=b+c$, $0<b\le c$, $c\ge A$, and
\[
                  c^2-bd=A,\qquad d^2-ce=-A.
\]
The first slope defect is $\delta=1$ for $\alpha\ge2$ and two for
$\alpha=1$. The next slope defect $\eta$ is exactly
\[
 \eta=\begin{cases}g+1,&\alpha\ge3,\\g+2,&\alpha=2,\\2g+1,&\alpha=1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Indeed the first two ordinary defects are $\delta$ and
$g\delta+3-\mathbf1_{\alpha\ge3}$, from the first two nonzero
coefficients of $H-[\alpha+1]$ and $K_1=g$.
Thus $\eta\ge g\delta+1$.
The first boundary minor is $A+\delta b>0$.
For the second, before taking positive parts, its minor is
\[
 (d-\delta)^2-c(e-\eta)
       =-A-2\delta d+\delta^2+c\eta
       \ge(c-A)+\delta(W-3)c+\delta^2\ge\delta^2>0.       \tag{3}
\]
Here $d\le2c$ and $W\ge3$. Its middle entry $d-\delta$ is positive.
If the next entry is negative, replacing it by zero still gives
a strictly positive minor; otherwise (3) applies unchanged.
This proves both positive-part seed comparisons.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Open generic two-seed closure.}
For ANY positive LC polynomial $L$ of degree $2\le h\le m$,
consider (1), allowing every $\alpha\ge1$ and the infinite-padding
case. The proposed assertion is:
\[
 \{A_m^2\ge A_{m-1}A_{m+1},\quad
   A_{m+1}^2\ge A_mA_{m+2}\}
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 A_n^2\ge A_{n-1}A_{n+1}\ \ (n\ge m).                  \tag{TSC}
\]
This is NOT proved. No claim of arbitrary convolution preserving
a fixed LC tail is made. If (TSC) is proved, the proposition supplies
its premises for EVERY actual CI entrance. The proved $h+1$ finite
principle would then give all actual late C for $u\ge h+1$.
Earlier reference C, shorter binary counts and the remaining
original mixed/incoming/A2 branches would STILL be open.

An exact finite diagnostic samples 100,000 arbitrary LC integer
numerators generated by nonincreasing integer slopes in base-two
logarithms. Among 12,141 satisfying BOTH seed inequalities, no later
LC failure occurs. These are finite nonfailures, not a closure theorem.
Separate earlier infinite-padding diagnostics also found no failure;
they are not used as proof or as additional certified counts.

\paragraph{Why the seeds and the factor structure cannot be dropped.}
For $L=(1,3,7,15,31,63,127,63,31,15,7,3,1)$, its positive
rising primitive is $(1,2,4,8,16,32,64)$, hence LC as well.
At $h=12$, $u=16$ the inverse-transform triple at $j=27$ is
$(47034,29069,17966)$ with minor $-6083$.
Thus ordinary LC, symmetry and LC of the positive primitive do
not by themselves supply the entrance. This is a NON-CI example.
The two-seed premises in (TSC) must be retained and proved from
actual capacities, as done above, if that proposed closure is used.

LC of $L$ cannot simply be dropped either. The NON-LC numerator
\[
 (8382,5500,3637,6405,4496,3336,1145,7890,263,
 8586,7780,899,1566,6653,3110,8236,7298,3313)
\]
has degree seventeen. At $m=19$ with infinite padding, its two
first minors are $687189124625$ and $16793581052289$, both positive,
but it fails LC at ranks $32,33,34,35,39,40$. This is a negative
control, not a counterexample to the LC-numerator assertion (TSC).

\paragraph{Formal and computational scope.}
\texttt{BinaryCITwoSeed.lean} checks the strict first Cassini payment,
the FULL arbitrary-parameter second-seed lower bound (3), and strict
positive-part transport. Actual polynomial identification (2), source
jets and mode joining are written arguments. The separate exact QA
rebuilds signed actual tails, including negative coefficients and every
boundary read. None of these proves the proposed all-tail closure.
