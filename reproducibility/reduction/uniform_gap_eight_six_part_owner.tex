\section{Sharp uniform source gap eight when the owner has at least six parts}
\begin{theorem}[Gap eight, with unrestricted first missing weight]
Let $Z=1^\alpha U$ have $h=|U|\ge6$ non-one parts, and let $Y$
have at least two. Assume $F_Z(j+1)\le F_Z(j)$ and that $E_Y$
has nondecreasing prefix through $j+1$. If
\[
                         \alpha\ge\kappa_Y-8,         \tag{1}
\]
then $e_{n+1}\le e_n$. Thus all-head S holds with the usual
OLD A2 head input, without C or a first-half premise.
The source gap eight is optimal uniformly over $h\ge6$.
The exact initial exchange permits replacing $\alpha$ in (1)
by $\alpha_* =\max(\alpha,t_1-1)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $d=\kappa_Y-\alpha$. For $d\le6$ the previous gap-six
theorem applies. If $d=7$ and $\alpha=1$, then $\kappa_Y=8$
and the absolute threshold-eight theorem applies.
It remains to prove
\[
              d=7,\ \alpha\ge2;\qquad d=8,\ \alpha\ge1.         \tag{2}
\]
In both ranges $\kappa_Y\ge9$. Therefore the SAME compulsory
source increments $w_0\ge1$, $w_t\ge2^{t-2}$ for $2\le t\le6$
are available, independently of the initial run and capacities.

If $r=a-j\le-2$, the tail cannot recover. If $r=-1$, the first
birth has index $D+h+2-d\ge6$, so pays the unit curvature.
For positive recovery at $r\ge0$, use the preceding complete
degree/primitive reductions:
\[
 e=D-2r-2\ge0,\quad C=d_{r+1}-d_r>0,\quad
 (r+1)C\le(e+1)d_r,\quad
 D\le\min\{2d(d-3),12d-25\}.
\]
As before $s=d-e-2\le0$ is directly paid by the first birth.
For $L=s-1=d-e-3\ge0$, use the EXACT rational CI/source-sum
test (2) of \texttt{kappa\_nine\_source\_nonrecovery.tex}.
This test depends only on $d,r,e$, not on $\alpha$ or the host.
Its complete finite rank domain has $D\ge h\ge6$.

For gap seven the rational test pays 98 rank states and leaves
30 states, with maximum $D=19$. For gap eight it pays 146
rank states and leaves 52 states, with maximum $D=25$.
These are ALL rank states permitted by the unbounded bounds,
not a chosen numerical window.

For every remaining state enumerate ALL partitions of $D$ into
$h\ge6$ positive increments $t_i-1$. No first-capacity restriction
is imposed. Let
\[
 b_0=D-r+h+1-d,\qquad
 N=\max\{\alpha_{\min},b_0,r+1\},
 \quad\alpha_{\min}=2\ (d=7),\quad1\ (d=8).
\]
Only $q_Z$ through $r+1$ and $F_Z$ through $b_0$ are used.
The exact initial-run clipping lemma implies that all $\alpha\ge N$
agree on both prefixes. Indeed their initial interval agrees at all
these ranks, and every terminal reverse source changed by clipping
is still above $r+1$. Therefore
$\alpha_{\min}\le\alpha\le N$ covers EVERY initial run exactly.

At ALL resulting critical states the direct source bound satisfies
\[
                    F_Z(b_0)+\sum_{t=2}^{6}2^{t-2}F_Z(b_0-t)\ge C.       \tag{3}
\]
For gap seven this is 20245 complete initial-clamped owners,
including 16470 strict-curvature states. For gap eight it is
187147 complete owners, including 133809 strict-curvature states.
An independent maximum-part enumeration, reverse polynomial
construction and individual-ratio calculation reconstructs ALL
82 layers, every count and every critical coefficient.
The canonical critical hashes are respectively
\[
\begin{split}
&\texttt{54963c84ae5f2f631d9d4c623e1c2f3f9b9b5ba830f2cf252326c0e1f0a581e9},\\
&\texttt{18169510425e7a490a5136c42ee2388195da8334a43f40603548f273a1e2c9f8}.
\end{split}
\]
Equation (3) lower-bounds the actual source difference through
the genuine compulsory increments, with zero extension outside
the ordinary support. It pays every remaining curvature and
contradicts recovery. Together with the earlier branches, this
proves (1) for ALL initial runs, source weights, counts and capacities.
\end{proof}

For sharpness, for every $\alpha\ge2$ put
\[
 Z=1^\alpha2^6,\quad Y=1(\alpha+8)^g,
 \quad g\ge\alpha+11,\qquad j=\alpha+11.
\]
The opposite terminal block agrees locally with its renewal
reference, $\kappa_Y=\alpha+9$, and its source prefix is
nondecreasing at all used indices. Direct low-coefficient calculation gives
\[
 d_1=6,\quad d_2=15,\quad
 (E_YF_Z)_{j+1}=11,\quad(E_YF_Z)_j=5,
 \qquad e_n=17,\quad e_{n+1}=20.
\]
Thus gap nine cannot replace eight. Head one has exact budget
$g^2+14g+35>3$, so this family does not refute S or original UI.
The restriction $\alpha\ge2$ matters for the quoted tail values:
at $\alpha=1$ the degree-two reverse primitive is fourteen,
and the earlier absolute-threshold sharpness family recovers
from seventeen to nineteen instead.

The generic unbounded source-sum/CI payment, clipping and degree
steps reuse the existing scoped Lean proofs; no new distinct
kernel report is counted for this theorem. Its finite complement
is independently exact-verified by the two separate programs,
not end-to-end Lean verified. The preceding absolute threshold-nine
theorem is also an immediate corollary, since $\alpha\ge1$.

After canonical exchange, any positive mixed recovery with $h\ge6$
and nondecreasing opposite source prefix must now satisfy
$\kappa_Y\ge\alpha_*+9\ge10$. Smaller owners retain their
previous sharp constants. The general mixed head comparison outside
these ranges, source-prefix decline, both-rising late overlap,
general C/A2 and original unamplified coefficient UI remain OPEN.
