\paragraph{Windows with one exceptional initial primitive centre.}

applies to this truncated primitive. At $n=M$, lowering its next
primitive by the incoming negative difference improves the minor.
For $n\ge\max(M+1,t+1)$ use the registered Frechet proof with

Thus $(a_n+R_{n-t}-M_a)^+$ has the required LC. This proves the
weakened window lemma with no assumption at primitive centre one.

\paragraph{A doubled robust head.}

\[
 M_B(s)\ge2q^s(B_{s+1}+2qB_s+q^2B_{s-1})
                                \quad(2\le s\le u-2). \tag{1}
\]

This proves $K(s,k)\ge2q^sT_k$ for every $k\ge1$.
For row zero it suffices to prove
$Q_s\ge q^s(3u-3+2q)$. At $s=2$ use the quadratic binomial lower

\paragraph{Fixed-$u$ interval induction for $u\ge12$.}
The empty-tail base has its ENTIRE positive primitive LC by the
registered binary base (it applies for every $u\ge3$).
Append $L'=L_0[t]$ with old length $r$, $u\ge\deg L_0+t\ge2r+3$.
Let $B=L_0Q_u$, $a=\phi^2\psi^uL'(-\phi)$,
$X_n=B_n-a\psi^n$. The exact interval norm bound
$|[t](-\phi)|q^{t-1}\le2q^2<1$ gives
\[
 |a|\le2q,\qquad |a|\le4q^3<1\quad\hbox{if }r\ge1.
\]
The doubled budget (1) proves $X$ LC at centres $2,\ldots,u-2$.
Its read entries at ranks $1,\ldots,u-1$ are positive: at ranks
through $u-2$ use $Q_n\ge u-q$; at $u-1$ use
$Q_{u-1}>3/2$ and $|a\psi^{u-1}|\le2q^u<1$.
The exact primitive identities are
\[
 d_n=X_n\ (1\le n<t),\quad d_t=X_t-1,\quad
                              d_{t+1}=X_{t+1}-B_1.   \tag{2}
\]
They are used only through index $u$ when available. The quotient
correction to $[t]J_{L_0}$ has degree $t-2$, so the weakened window
lemma proves all primitive centres at least $t+1$.
Centres $2,\ldots,t-2$ use (1); at $t-1$ lowering the next value by
one improves the minor. There is no need to pay primitive centre one.

If $t\le u-2$, write $b=B_1=u+r-q$ and
$D=b^2-2B_2=u-r-q^2$. Ordinary LC gives
$bB_t\le B_2B_{t-1}$ and
$B_{t-1}\ge(r+1)(u-q)\ge(r+1)(u-1)$.
For $r\ge1$, the concave scalar inequality on $1\le r\le(u-3)/2$
\[
 (u-r-1)(r+1)(u-1)\ge u(u+r)
\]
has endpoint margins $u^2-7u+4$ and
$(u-1)(u^2-6u-1)/4$, both positive for $u\ge12$.
It implies $bB_{t-1}-2B_t\ge u$.
Its perturbation error is at most
$q^2(b+2q)\le(2/5)(3u/2)=3u/5$.
Thus $bX_{t-1}-2X_t\ge2u/5$.

Global ordinary LC of $J$ gives initial-interval support of its
positive primitive; all clipping/zero cases therefore follow.

an endpoint with next positive part zero; earlier centres were paid
