\section{A probability renewal identity gives a strict rounding bound at EVERY order}
Let $t\ge2$, let $q\in(0,1)$ be the unique root
$q+q^2+\cdots+q^t=1$, and put $\lambda=1/q\in(1,2)$.
Write
\[
 H_t=(1-z-\cdots-z^t)^{-1}=\sum_{n\ge0}h_nz^n,\qquad
 G_t=H_t/(1-z)=\sum_{n\ge0}g_nz^n.
\]
Negative coefficients are zero. Define positive probability weights
\[
 a_i=q^{t-i+1}\ (1\le i\le t),\qquad
 A(z)=\sum_{i=1}^t a_i z^i,\qquad
 B(z)=\sum_{i=0}^{t-1}\left(\sum_{k=1}^{t-i}q^k\right)z^i.
\]
The weights sum to one. The COMPLETE polynomial identities are
\[
 1-z-\cdots-z^t=(1-\lambda z)B(z),\qquad
 (1-z)B(z)=1-A(z).                              \tag{1}
\]
For the first, $b_0=1$, $b_i-\lambda b_{i-1}=-1$ and
$\lambda b_{t-1}=1$. The second follows from
$b_{i-1}-b_i=q^{t-i+1}$, including the last coefficient $-q$.
Equivalently $B=\sum_i a_i[i]$; the generic whole polynomial
mixture identity is one of the new kernel reports.

Let $U=(1-A)^{-1}$. It obeys the positive finite renewal recurrence
$U_0=1$, $U_n=\sum_i a_iU_{n-i}$. For EVERY $n\ge1$,
\[
                         0<U_n<1.              \tag{2}
\]
Indeed $U_n>0$ follows from the $i=1$ term and induction.
All old reads lie in $[0,1]$, giving $U_n\le1$.
For $n=1$, the positive $i=2$ weight reads a negative index and
is lost, so the inequality is strict; for $n\ge2$ the positive
$i=1$ term reads the strictly smaller old value $U_{n-1}<1$.
This is an all-rank convex-combination induction, not a root
approximation or an asymptotic estimate.

From (1), EXACTLY as formal power series,
\[
 (1-\lambda z)G_t=U,\qquad (1-\lambda z)H_t=(1-z)U.
\]
Thus at every $n\ge0$, and respectively every $n\ge1$,
\[
 g_n-\lambda g_{n-1}=U_n\in(0,1],\qquad
             -1<h_n-\lambda h_{n-1}<1.          \tag{3}
\]
There is no finite exceptional band, no bound on $t$, and no
unproved irreducibility, conjugate-root or spectral-decay premise.
The normalization at rank zero is retained: $U_0=1$ and the
second difference at rank zero is one, so its strict bound starts
at rank ONE. The unbounded renewal bounds and signed increments
are kernel checked in \texttt{UniversalRenewalRounding.lean}.

\section{A universal first-defect ordinary-coefficient criterion for actual C}
\begin{theorem}
Let $X$ be ANY normalized nonrectangular increasing arm of maximum
capacity $t\ge2$, with actual forward coefficients $F$, positive
differences $D_n=(F_n-F_{n-1})^+$, $D_0=D_1=0$, and cumulatives
$G$. Let $L$ be the FIRST mode of $D$, and let $a$ be the first
rank at which $F$ differs from $H_t$. If
\[
 F_n^2\ge F_{n-1}F_{n+1}\qquad(a\le n<L),       \tag{4}
\]
then $X$ has COMPLETE C at every current rank and every shift.
All capacities, counts, padding and ranks are arbitrary. The
premise is ordinary coefficient LC, not cumulative LC or D LC.
For actual arms it is equivalent to ordinary F LC at every centre
$n\ge a$, because the tail from $L$ is already automatically LC.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every composition under $X$ uses parts at most $t$, so $F_n\le h_n$.
The first difference occurs at a finite rank $a\ge3$, and by
integrality $F_a\le h_a-1$. The entire earlier prefix is equal.
The universal strict bound (3) gives the ACTUAL first ratio entry
\[
                  F_a<\lambda F_{a-1}.         \tag{5}
\]
All protected earlier cumulative ratios are greater than $\lambda$
by (3). Every C comparison at current $N<a$ is the already proved
alphabet-reference C of \texttt{first\_missing\_renewal\_envelope.tex}.
At $N=a$, only the current positive derivative is lowered, so
those comparisons remain true, even if that derivative vanishes.

For $a<N\le L$ discard the automatic case $D_N\le D_{N-1}$.
Otherwise put $\mu=D_N/D_{N-1}>1$. Hypothesis (4) pays precisely
the whole bounded ordinary-to-cumulative bridge from
\texttt{ordinary\_tail\_to\_complete\_C\_proof.tex}, with
$K=N-a-1$, $f_j=F_{a-1+j}$, $g_j=G_{a-2+j}$.
The ordinary LC minors propagate the last derivative ratio
backwards; (5) gives $\mu<\lambda$.
The protected cumulative entry is greater than $\lambda$ and
each later cumulative gap propagates by
\[
 (G_{j+1}-\mu G_j)-(G_j-\mu G_{j-1})
                              =F_{j+1}-\mu F_j\ge0.
\]
Consequently every required $G_kD_{N-1}\ge G_{k-1}D_N$
($1\le k\le N-3$) holds. Earlier protected $k$ use (3) directly.
After $L$, the actual positive derivative is nonincreasing and
C is automatic. This includes $L\le a$, when the ordinary
hypothesis is empty. The automatic ordinary-LC tail after the
first D mode is the existing actual reverse-rising LC and
nonincreasing-forward-slopes argument, valid for ANY arm.
\end{proof}
