\section{Unbounded first-missing mode bounds and the entire one-part window case}
All arms below are ACTUAL normalized increasing arms. No fixed
upper bound on capacities, counts, padding, or first missing weight
is imposed. The general original coefficient conjecture remains OPEN.

\begin{theorem}[A universal ordinary-product mode bound]
Let $X,Y$ be nonrectangular arms, and let $\kappa=\kappa_Y\ge6$.
Write $H=F_XF_Y$, $D=\operatorname{wt}X+\operatorname{wt}Y$.
Then
\[
 H_\kappa>H_{\kappa-1},\qquad H_{\kappa+1}>H_\kappa,
 \qquad H_{\kappa+2}>H_{\kappa+1}.
\]
Consequently the FIRST ordinary mode of $H$ is at least $\kappa+2$,
and the LAST ordinary mode of $q_Xq_Y$ is at most $D-\kappa-2$.
No C, A2, or comparison-mode hypothesis is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $A=F_X$, $B=F_Y$, with zero extension at negative indices.
The actual normalized prefix has $A_0=A_1=1$, $A_2=2$,
$A_3\ge1$, and all later coefficients are nonnegative.
The first-missing prefix gives $B_0=1$, $B_j=2^{j-1}$ for
$1\le j<\kappa$, and
\[
 B_\kappa=2^{\kappa-1}-\delta,\qquad1\le\delta\le\kappa.
\]
The last inequality is the frozen first-missing-hook theorem: ALL
missing words at the first missing rank are hooks. Also $B_j>0$
through rank $\kappa+2$, because the compulsory triangular weight
is at least $\kappa(\kappa-1)/2\ge\kappa+2$.

The COMPLETE ordinary-product difference identity is
\[
 \Delta H_N=B_N+B_{N-2}-2B_{N-3}
                 +\sum_{i\ge3}A_i\Delta B_{N-i}.       \tag{1}
\]
For $N=\kappa,\kappa+1$, every read in the sum is within the
protected nondecreasing prefix of $B$, and the two explicit powers
cancel. Hence $\Delta H_N\ge B_N>0$.
At $N=\kappa+2$ the explicit second pair is $-\delta$.
All sum terms remain in the protected prefix, and its $i=3$ term
is at least $2^{\kappa-3}$. The elementary unbounded induction
$2^{\kappa-3}\ge\kappa$ for $\kappa\ge6$ therefore gives
\[
       \Delta H_{\kappa+2}\ge B_{\kappa+2}
                              +2^{\kappa-3}-\delta>0.  \tag{2}
\]
Ordinary actual-product unimodality puts EVERY mode at or after
the last strict increase. Reversal interchanges first and last
modes, giving the claimed reverse bound.

For formalization write $A=1+z+2z^2+z^3U$, with $U\ge0$ and
$U_0\ge1$. The kernel proves the WHOLE polynomial identity (1),
nonnegativity of the entire protected difference prefix, complete
convolution nonnegativity there, its constant-term lower bound,
and ALL THREE increases together. No source sum is assumed positive
without its full used-index guard.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Every one-part-owner source window at arbitrary first missing weight]
Let $X=1^\alpha s$, $\alpha\ge1,s\ge2$, and let $Y$ be ANY
nonrectangular actual arm with FULL C. Assume FULL OLD A2 for
the pair $q_Xq_Y$. Write $\kappa=\kappa_Y$ and $E=(2-z)q_Xq_Y$.
Then for EVERY integer $t\ge\max(1,\kappa-3)$, the positive part
of the ENTIRE signed source window $[t]E$ is unimodal and has
initial positive support. This covers ALL first missing weights,
not merely the previously classified values through eight.
The FULL OLD A2 premise is explicitly retained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
One-part $X$ has known FULL C and first missing weight three.
The old both-rising C resource, both-nonrising actual resources,
and A1 apply. In a mixed comparison with owner $Y$, the rising
opposite has first missing weight three, and its old pointwise
birth theorem pays all comparisons. With owner $X$, its exact
reverse polynomial and positive primitive are
\[
 q_X=[\alpha+1][s]+z^a,\quad a=\alpha+s,\qquad
 (\Delta q_X(r))^+=\mathbf1_{0\le r<m},\quad
 m=\min(\alpha+1,s).
\]
Positive primitive curvature occurs ONLY at $r=-1$. Its compulsory
birth is $A_{a+2-\kappa}$, which pays whenever $\kappa\le a+2$.
All these cases have whole raw-source positive UI by the frozen
FULL OLD A2/plateau/Q/A1 assembly, and EVERY window is allowed.
This also covers $\kappa\le5$ since $a\ge3$.

The ONLY remaining case is
\[
 a+3\le\kappa,\qquad\kappa\ge6.
\]
Write $b=\operatorname{wt}Y$, $D=a+b$, and set
\[
 L=b+a-\kappa+1,\qquad R=b+m-1.
\]
The ONLY possible Q exception is at $b-1\to b$. The preceding
unbounded mode theorem puts the reverse LAST ordinary mode at
most $D-\kappa-2=L-3$. Thus FULL OLD A2, the whole plateau join,
and the paid Q edges prove UI of the source prefix $0,\ldots,L$.
Its endpoint is $E_L=\delta\ge1$ and $E_0=2$; A1 and Q rule out
an earlier negative or zero followed by this positive endpoint.
The entire prefix is positive integer, hence at least one.

The exact forward reciprocal coefficient identity is
\[
 E_{D-k}=A_k-A_{k+1}+(A E_Y)_{k+1}.
\]
It gives the COMPLETE remaining source profile
\[
 E_{L+1}=\cdots=E_{b-1}=0,\qquad
 E_b=\cdots=E_R=1,\qquad E_n\le0\quad(n>R).           \tag{3}
\]
The gap has length $\kappa-a-2\ge1$. For $n\ge b$ the whole
ordinary read is below the first source birth, so (3) is exactly
the one-part primitive, including its negative terminal unit.

The generic finite-height/width patch theorem applies with
$B=L$ and $H=1$. Its complete reach condition is
\[
 R-L=\kappa-a+m-2\le\kappa-3\le t,
\]
since $m\le a-1$. Every mode and subtraction index here is valid:
$b\ge\kappa$, $a\ge3$, and $a+3\le\kappa$ give $1\le L<R$.
Hence the ENTIRE positive window is UI with initial support.
No claim that raw-source UI holds across the actual zero gap is used.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{An unbounded actual shorter-window obstruction.}
For EVERY $\kappa\ge6$, take $X=(1,2)$ and
$Y=1,(\kappa-1)^{\kappa-2}$. Its first missing rank is $\kappa$.
Every composition of smaller weight embeds. At weight $\kappa$
the ONLY missing words are
\[
 (\kappa),\qquad(2,1^{\kappa-2}),\qquad1^\kappa.
\]
Indeed a word whose first part exceeds one has length at most
$\kappa-2$ unless it is the second displayed hook; a word starting
in one uses the initial one and fits the remaining constant
capacities unless it is $1^\kappa$; a part above $\kappa-1$ forces
the first displayed singleton. Thus $\delta=3$ exactly.
Profile (3), which itself does not require C or A2, gives
\[
 ([\kappa-4]E)_{b-1},([\kappa-4]E)_b,
 ([\kappa-4]E)_{b+1}=(3,1,2).
\]
This is an unconditional actual source-window counterexample for
arbitrarily large first missing weight. C and FULL OLD A2 for this
entire unbounded counterexample family are NOT asserted here.
The abstract patch length cannot be shortened using only its
positive-UI-prefix and critical-block hypotheses: the same pattern
is produced by a prefix $(2,3,\ldots,3)$ followed by the displayed
zeros, two ones, and a nonpositive finite tail.
