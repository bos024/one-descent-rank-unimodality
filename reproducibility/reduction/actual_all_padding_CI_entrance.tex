\section{Every actual CI entrance and all-padding late C: proof under audit}
For EVERY nonempty sorted tail $V$ with capacities at least three,
degree $h$, length $r$, weight $W=h+r$, EVERY padding $\alpha\ge1$
and EVERY binary count $u\ge h+1$, the proposed theorem is:
actual positive slopes of $1^\alpha2^uV$ are LC at every moving centre
\[
                            u+r+1\le N<\ell,
\]
where $\ell$ is their first global mode; EVERY actual C comparison
holds at EVERY current rank $N\ge W+1$ and EVERY shift $R\ge3$.
The arbitrary-padding single entrance is therefore supplied, rather
than retained as a hypothesis. This is still a LATE-C theorem:
earlier reference C, smaller binary counts, general C/A2 and original
unamplified UI require separate work. The proof below is under audit.

\paragraph{The formal coefficient entrance.}
Let $m=u-1\ge h$, $C=L\sum_{k=0}^m(z+z^2)^k$,
$E=(1+z)^uL$, $a=\alpha-1$, and
$A_n=(C_n-E_{n-u-a})^+$. Write
\[
 b_s=\phi C_{u+s-1}-C_{u+s}
   =[z^s]\frac{(1+z)^uL-c_0}{1+qz},\quad
                  c_0=\psi^uL(-\phi).
\]
The registered entire-tail theorem makes $b$ positive finite global
LC. The new repaired primitive theorem makes its positive primitive
LC after deleting rank zero. The backward finite identity gives
\[
 x_s:=\sum_{j\ge1}q^j b_{s+j}=C_{u+s}\quad(s\ge0).
\]
It also holds at $s=-1$ by $C_{u-1}=q(C_u+b_0)$.
The TRUE source differs from $b_s+qb_{s-1}$ ONLY at rank zero:
\[
                 E=(1+qz)b+c_0.                     \tag{1}
\]
Hence the generic geometric cut and this actual signed source differ
only at coefficient offset $s=a$. Their triples agree at every centre
$s\ge a+2$. The signed-primitive cut theorem proves coefficient LC
there whenever $s<M$, with $M$ the first $b$ mode.

\paragraph{The first mode lies inside this exact range.}
The source $z^{m+\alpha}(1+z)^{m+1}$ dominates EACH row
$z^k(1+z)^k$, $k\le m$, in likelihood-ratio order: both support
endpoints are later and on overlap its successive ratio is larger.
Sum the comparisons over $k$. Convolution with LC $L$ preserves this
order, by its Toeplitz $2$ by $2$ minors. Thus the true source density
relative to $C$ is nondecreasing. The positive part $A$ has initial
interval support, and its first global mode $e$ is no later than the
first $C$ mode. Before $m$ there is no subtraction and $C$ strictly
grows, so $e\ge m$.

At the first $b$ mode, $x_M\le\sum_{j\ge1}q^jb_M=\phi b_M$.
Thus $x_M-x_{M-1}=q^2x_M-qb_M\le0$. For $M=0$ the needed
$x_{-1}$ identification was established above. Since $C$ is LC from
centre $m=u-1$ onward, its first mode is at most $u+M$.
Therefore $e-u\le M$. Every centre before $e$ and after the startup
has $s<M$, exactly as required by the cut theorem. The minor at the
global first mode $e$ is automatic.

\paragraph{Uniform payments at the source startup.}
Put $w=E_1=u+r$ and $E_2=(w^2-u+r)/2$.
Assume first $w\ge8$. All coefficient ratios of $C$ from $u$ onward
are less than $\phi$; its ordinary minors from $m$ are nonnegative.
Before offset $a-1$ the source is absent. At $a-1$ only the NEXT
coefficient is lowered by one, improving the $C$ minor.
At centre $a$, with $c=C_{u+a}$, $p=C_{u+a-1}$,
the exact signed minor is
\[
             M_C-2c+1+pw\ge M_C+p(w-2\phi)+1>0.
\]
At centre $a+1$, with $c=C_{u+a+1}$, $p=C_{u+a}$,
$n=C_{u+a+2}$, its signed minor is
\[
 M_C+pE_2-2wc+n+w^2-E_2
       \ge M_C+p(E_2-2\phi w)+n+w^2-E_2>0.
\]
Here $E_2\ge w(w-1)/2\ge(7/2)w>2\phi w$ and $E_2\le w^2$.
If a startup centre occurs before the first $A$ mode, the preceding
and middle coefficients are positive by initial-interval support.
If the raw next coefficient is negative, its positive part is zero
and the coefficient minor is a square, hence nonnegative. Otherwise
the displayed signed minor applies unchanged.
Startup centres below $m$ are not needed. In particular, when
$\alpha=1$ the centre $m=u-1$ is paid simply by lowering its next
coefficient; the exceptional $s=0$ deficit value is never substituted
into a primitive triple.

When $w<8$, $3\le u\le6$, $h\le u-1$ and $V$ has capacity at most
six. For each such $(u,V)$ the old first-mode bound
$\operatorname{mode}C\le\lceil(3m+h)/2\rceil$ makes EVERY
$\alpha\ge\lceil(m+h)/2\rceil+1$ identical to the infinite sequence
through its first mode, except a possible improvement of the next
value. Only $1\le\alpha\le\lceil(m+h)/2\rceil$ remain. These are
exactly FORTY-ONE contexts. All their pre-mode coefficient minors
are nonnegative; their 88 distinct integer margins are included in
the ordinary-kernel finite certificate. Thus the coefficient entrance
is proved at EVERY $m\ge h$ and EVERY padding, subject to the audits.

\paragraph{The actual model and every late C read.}
It suffices to use the SINGLE entrance $m=h$, $u=h+1$.
The exact signed actual derivative agrees with $C^\alpha$ at physical
rank $r+2+n$ for every $n\ge h-1$. The strict edge at $n=m$, together
with the already proved actual positive-slope unimodality, puts its
first mode in this exact tail and identifies it with $e$ above.
All earlier formal values are smaller than $A_m$. The formal LC
entrance therefore supplies all actual centres starting at
$r+2+m=u+r+1=W+2$ through the first actual mode.

The proved fixed-padding count induction propagates this entrance at
EACH padding separately to EVERY $u\ge h+1$. The complete homogeneous
reference C theorem from $W+1$, the first-defect improvement, TWO
ordinary-F seeds and the existing finite D/F/G read integration pay
EVERY shift $R\ge3$ at EVERY $N\ge W+1$.
No premise of general C, A2 or original one-descent UI has been inserted
into this argument. Its inputs are the registered ordinary-arm
resources and the new repaired primitive/cut theorems.

\paragraph{What this does not settle.}
Even when the present audits pass, earlier reference ranks $N\le W$
and shorter binary counts $u<h+1$ remain unresolved in general.
Those, and the recorded original mixed/incoming/A2 branches, must
still be proved. The original general unamplified conjecture is not
declared solved by the all-padding entrance or late-C statement.
