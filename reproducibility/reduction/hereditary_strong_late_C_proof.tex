\section{A hereditary strong late-C entrance and unrestricted sorted suffixes}
Let $F$ be the ordinary rank sequence of an actual normalized sorted arm
$1^\alpha2^uV$. Write $h=\sum_{t\in V}(t-1)$, $W=\sum V$,
$r=|V|$, $w=u+r$, $q=\phi-1$, and $c=q^2=2-\phi$.
Cumulatives are $G_k=\sum_{j\le k}F_j$, zero below zero and constant
after the ordinary support. Signed slopes are $d_n=F_n-F_{n-1}$,
and positive slopes $D_n=d_n^+$ have $D_0=D_1=0$.
All conclusions below concern actual finite padding, with all capacities,
counts and ranks unbounded.

We use the registered actual ordinary and positive-slope unimodality,
strict positive-slope support, ordinary tail LC, and universal positive-D
LC before its first mode at centres $W+1$ and later when $h>u$.
The ordinary tail-LC input includes its actual first-mode boundary.
No global ordinary LC or finite/infinite relative LC is assumed.

\paragraph{The conditional strong entrance.}
For an arm with $h>u$, call its physical entrance strong if, WHEN
$D_{W+1}>D_W>0$, its ratio $\lambda=D_{W+1}/D_W$ satisfies
\[
 \lambda<\phi,\qquad
 F_n^2\ge F_{n-1}F_{n+1}\quad(n\ge W),\qquad
 G_k-\lambda G_{k-1}\ge c\quad(1\le k\le W).       \tag{1}
\]
When the entrance is flat, falling or clipped, (1) imposes no
ordinary-LC or positive-ratio assertion. Universal pre-mode D-LC
and D-unimodality imply all later D pairs are nonincreasing,
so every later C comparison is automatic in this branch.
A pre-mode flat is excluded using LC, not unimodality alone.

A strong entrance implies every C comparison from current $W+1$.
Indeed, at a later strictly growing current $N$, use its OWN ratio
$\mu=D_N/D_{N-1}\le\lambda$. Every earlier positive-D ratio from
$W+1$ through $N$ is at least $\mu$, by pre-first-mode D-LC.
Ordinary LC at centre $W$ is exactly
$D_WF_W-D_{W+1}F_{W-1}\ge0$, so $F_W-\mu F_{W-1}\ge0$.
Integrate the nonnegative $d_j-\mu d_{j-1}$ to extend this ordinary
comparison, then integrate once more to extend the cumulative margin.
The paid prefix in (1) therefore supplies every C read, with additive
margin at least $c$ whenever the current pair strictly grows.
Falling current pairs and zero support are immediate.

\paragraph{A quantitative reference margin strengthens the existing seeds.}
For the infinite binary cumulative $g_n=\operatorname{Fib}(n+3)-1$,
set $K_0=1$, $K_n=g_n-\phi g_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$.
Its exact recurrence is $K_{n+1}=1-qK_n$. The interval $[q^2,1]$
is invariant: if $q^2\le K\le1$, then
$1-qK\ge1-q=q^2$ and $1-qK\le1$.
Thus every positive reference read has golden margin at least $c$.

An append $t\ge3$ gives $g'_k=1+\sum_{i=1}^t g_{k-i}$.
Its golden residual is $-q$ plus the sum of the old residuals.
For $1\le k\le t$ the rank-zero residual contributes one, paying
the atom and leaving at least $1-q=c$; all other reads are nonnegative.
For $k>t$, there are $t$ positive reads, so the margin is at least
$tc-q\ge c$, since $t\ge3$ and $2q^2\ge q$.
Induction on the entire arbitrary capacity list proves
\[
                g_k-\phi g_{k-1}\ge c\quad(k\ge1).    \tag{2}
\]
The order need not be sorted for this reference statement.

The registered weaker count-cone proof supplies strong entrances at
EVERY seed with
\[
 r\ge2,\quad h-u\ge5,\quad W\ge33,\quad
                    17u+4r+30\ge13h.                \tag{3}
\]
This is a strengthening of its recorded margins, not an appeal to its
finite QA. At every unprotected read the proof pays the full source
with strictly positive surplus $711E_j/928$, IN ADDITION TO the
reference golden margin (2). Protected reads satisfy (2) directly.
Its two ordinary seeds preserve the same cumulative margin through
the last two reads. Its first ordinary seed gives LC at centre $W$.
Universal D-LC integrates ordinary LC up to the first D mode;
thereafter positive nonincreasing slopes give ordinary LC by concavity
until the ordinary mode, and registered ordinary tail LC covers the rest.
This establishes the ENTIRE ordinary LC tail in (1).
The low-degree and small-weight cases of the earlier count-cone theorem
are not silently assumed to have this stronger invariant; only (3) is
used as a new seed.

There are additional strong seeds at EVERY $u\ge33$, $1\le h-u\le4$,
including one-factor tails. Here is the complete low-gap argument.
Write $x=D^{\rm ref}_W$, $y=D^{\rm ref}_{W+1}$ and
$A=x^2+xy-y^2>0$. At gap one the full reference prefix through W
is retained, and the next derivative loses $c_0=1$ or2. The ordinary
seed is exactly $A+c_0y>0$, the ratio decreases below $y/x<\phi$,
and (2) gives every G margin without a lost prefix read.
At gap two the losses at W,W+1 are
$(b_0,c_0)=(2,2w+1)$ for alpha1, $(1,w+2)$ for alpha2,
and $(1,w+1)$ thereafter. Since $w\ge33$, $c_0\ge3b_0$.
The reference G prefix through W-1 is retained; the ordinary seed is
\[
 (x-b_0)(x+y-b_0)-(y-c_0)y
 =A+b_0^2+x(c_0-3b_0)+(y-x)(c_0-b_0)>0.
\]
It extends the unchanged cumulative margin to W and lowers the entry
ratio below phi. At gap three the TWO registered ordinary seeds in
the triple-subdegree proof extend its protected G prefix through W-2.
At gap four the THREE registered seeds in the four-subdegree proof
extend its protected prefix through W-3. Their hypotheses $w\ge6$
and $w\ge11$ follow from $u\ge33$. In each case the exact additive
margin (2), rather than merely nonnegativity, is preserved. The same
ordinary-seed/D-LC integration gives the full ordinary LC tail.

\paragraph{Hereditary theorem: every legal nonbinary append preserves (1).}
Suppose an arm has a strong entrance and append any $t\ge\max V$,
$t\ge3$. The exact actual recurrence is $F'=1+S_tF$.
Put $W'=W+t$, $a=W$, $b=W+t$. Its signed entrance pair is
\[
 A=d'_{W'}=F_{b-1}-F_{a-1},\qquad
 B=d'_{W'+1}=F_b-F_a.                              \tag{4}
\]
Only $B>A>0$ needs proof.

Ordinary unimodality and strict positive-slope support first imply
$d_a>0$: if rank $a$ had no positive slope, $F$ could not rise
between $a-1$ and $b-1$, contrary to $A>0$.
Equation (4) gives $d_b-d_a=B-A>0$, hence $d_b>d_a>0$.
D-unimodality makes EVERY signed slope on $[a,b]$ positive.
If the old entry were flat or falling, its universal D-LC and UI
would exclude this rise, so its conditional strong invariant applies.

Every old D ratio on $[a+1,b]$ is at most $\lambda$: use pre-mode
LC up to the first D mode and ratio at most one thereafter.
Sum these inequalities and use (4):
\[
                     \mu:=B/A\le\lambda<\phi.       \tag{5}
\]
This argument includes windows crossing the first D mode.

All ordinary values on $[a-1,b]$ are positive. Ordinary LC from
centre $a$ gives decreasing adjacent F ratios, in particular
$F_bF_{a-1}\le F_aF_{b-1}$. Exact subtraction then gives
\[
  B F_{b-1}\le A F_b,\qquad
              \mu\le F_b/F_{b-1}.                  \tag{6}
\]
Thus EVERY F ratio from index $a$ through $b$ is at least $\mu$.
This uses the upper endpoint ratio, rather than the latest D ratio.

By (5), every old G read through $W$ retains margin $c$ at ratio $\mu$.
For $W<k\le b-1$ use
\[
 (G_k-\mu G_{k-1})-(G_{k-1}-\mu G_{k-2})
                         =F_k-\mu F_{k-1}\ge0
\]
and (6). Hence every old G read needed by the new strong prefix
has the same additive margin. The exact cumulative identity gives
\[
 G'_k-\mu G'_{k-1}
       =1-\mu+\sum_{i=1}^t(G_{k-i}-\mu G_{k-i-1}).   \tag{7}
\]
For $1\le k\le t$ the sum contains the rank-zero term one,
leaving $2-\mu\ge2-\phi=c$. For $k>t$ it contains $t$ positive
old gaps, leaving at least $tc-(\mu-1)\ge tc-q\ge c$.
All other negative-index terms are exactly zero.
This proves every new strong G read through $W'$.

Finally truncate the old F below index $W-1$. The resulting sequence
is globally LC: all its interior centres are at least $W$, and its
new left endpoint is automatic. Its length-$t$ window agrees with
the new F at EVERY centre $n\ge W+t$, including both neighbouring
reads. Ordinary LC convolution therefore proves the new entire
ordinary LC tail from $W'$. This supplies the new ordinary seed.
Together with (5) and (7) it closes (1).
If the new entrance is not strictly growing, the automatic branch
applies. Thus induction over EVERY finite sorted suffix is valid.

Consequently, whenever ANY nonbinary prefix satisfies (3), the
whole actual sorted arm has every C shift at every current
\[
                         N\ge W_{\rm final}+1,      \tag{8}
\]
regardless of the suffix's number or size of parts. The final word
need not satisfy any count cone. The extra boundary $N=W_{\rm final}$
is not claimed by this hereditary argument.

\paragraph{An explicit new family and a constant-size suffix reduction.}
Let $u\ge33$ and let $d=\max V-1$ satisfy $13d\le4u+38$.
If $h\le u$, old results give late C. Otherwise take the first
nonbinary prefix whose degree $h_0\ge u+1$. Its overshoot is
$h_0\le u+d$. It has at least two factors since $d<u+1$.
Thus $13(h_0-u)\le13d\le4u+38\le4(u+r_0)+30$,
and $W_0\ge33$. It is a seed (3), so (8) follows.
For degree gaps one through four, use the additional low-gap seeds
just proved instead of the gap-at-least-five seed (3).
In fact the bound on $d$ is needed only through this crossing
prefix; the entire later suffix is arbitrary and sorted.

For general residual late C at $u\ge33$, impose the registered
automatic-complement bound $h\le3u-2$.
Let the first prefix of degree greater than $u$ end with capacity
$t_0=d_0+1$, and let $m$ count the subsequent nonbinary factors.
If this prefix has at least two factors and is not a seed, its
cone must fail:
\[
 4u+4r_0+30<13(h_0-u)\le13d_0.
\]
Sorting gives $h\ge u+1+md_0$. These two inequalities imply
$m\le6$: if $m\ge7$, then $7d_0\le2u-3$ whereas
$13d_0>4u+4r_0+30\ge4u+38$, which is impossible.
If the crossing prefix is a single factor, $d_0\ge u+1$ and
$(m+1)d_0\le3u-2$ instead imply $m\le1$.
Thus a possible remaining large-u late-C failure has AT MOST SIX
nonbinary factors after its first crossing prefix, or at most one
in the single-factor crossing case.
The crossing prefix and binary count are still unbounded.
This is not a finite completed verification and not a complete C theorem.

Twenty-four scoped Lean reports check the unbounded ratio/mode/sum/margin
arguments, full arbitrary-prefix and first-crossing inductions, and
the integer uniform-family/six-suffix guards. Actual recurrence,
support and global mode identification, LC-convolution application,
and the complete actual sequence assembly are the written proof above,
not an end-to-end Lean formalization of composition ideals.
All new finite profiles are independent QA only; no finite entrance
certificate is introduced. General earlier C, the unseeded crossing
entrance, full A2 and original unamplified coefficient unimodality
remain OPEN.
