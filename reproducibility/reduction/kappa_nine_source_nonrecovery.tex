\section{Every opposite first missing weight at most nine, for six-part owners}
\begin{theorem}[Sharp first-source threshold nine when $h\ge6$]
Let both actual increasing old arms have at least two non-one parts,
with named owner $Z=1^\alpha U$ having $h=|U|\ge6$.
If $F_Z(j+1)\le F_Z(j)$ and the opposite SOURCE $E_Y$ has
nondecreasing prefix through $j+1$, then EVERY
$\kappa_Y\le9$ gives tail nonrecovery $e_{n+1}\le e_n$.
Consequently all-head S follows with the usual OLD A2 head input,
without C or a first-half premise. Threshold nine is optimal for
this pointwise statement uniformly over $h\ge6$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The previous threshold-eight theorem handles $\kappa_Y\le8$.
Take $\kappa_Y=9$ and use the exact canonical initial exchange
$\alpha_* =\max(\alpha,t_1-1)$, keeping the same rank polynomial,
weight and non-one count. If $\alpha_*\ge3$, the sharp gap-six
theorem applies. Thus only the canonical cases
\[
        \alpha=1,\quad t_1=2;\qquad
        \alpha=2,\quad t_1\le3                         \tag{1}
\]
remain. Here $d=9-\alpha$ is eight or seven.
Negative reverse ranks are paid by the first birth:
at $r=-1$ its forward index is $D+h+2-d\ge0$ because
$D\ge h\ge6$; for $r\le-2$ there is no primitive curvature.

Suppose $r\ge0$ and the tail recovers. The established skeleton
and source reductions give
\[
 e=D-2r-2\ge0,\quad C=d_{r+1}-d_r>0,\quad
 (r+1)C\le(e+1)d_r,
 \quad D\le\min\{2d(d-3),12d-25\}.
\]
The $s=d-e-2\le0$ cases are already paid by the first birth.
Otherwise $L=s-1=d-e-3\ge0$ and $0\le e\le d-3$.
For $0\le m\le r$ put
\[
 R_m=\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}\frac{r+e+3+i}{r-i}
     =\frac{{r+e+m+2\choose m}}{{r\choose m}}.
\]
The source has compulsory increments $w_0\ge1$ and
$w_t\ge2^{t-2}$ for $2\le t\le6$, relative to its first birth.
For every included $t$ with $L+t\le r$, lower-center reflection
and the exact CI backward ratios give
\[
 F_Z(j+1-9-t)\ge d_{r-L-t}\ge d_r/R_{L+t}.
\]
Thus the ENTIRE source difference pays $C$ whenever
\[
 \mathbf1_{L\le r}/R_L+
 \sum_{\substack{2\le t\le6\\L+t\le r}}
                    2^{t-2}/R_{L+t}\ \ge\ \frac{e+1}{r+1}.       \tag{2}
\]
Absent terms are zero. Each retained birth is within the assumed
source prefix because its forward index is at least $h$.

The proved unbounded owner bounds reduce the residual geometry to
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 \alpha&d&e&D\\\hline
 1&8&0,\ldots,5&6\le D\le71,\\
 2&7&0,\ldots,4&6\le D\le56,
 \end{array}\qquad D=2r+2+e.                         \tag{3}
\]
Evaluate (2) in exact rationals throughout (3): it pays 244
complete rank states. Independent products of individual fractions
agree with the binomial-ratio evaluation at every state.
Exactly 82 rank states remain, with maximum $D$ only 25.

For each of these 82 states enumerate ALL partitions of $D$ into
$h\ge6$ positive capacity increments. In (1) their minimum is
at most $\alpha$. There are exactly 13544 owners across these
states, of which 2464 have strict positive primitive curvature.
Every critical owner satisfies
\[
 F_Z(b_0)+\sum_{t=2}^{6}2^{t-2}F_Z(b_0-t)\ge C,
               \qquad b_0=D-r+h+1-d.                 \tag{4}
\]
Use zero extension at negative indices. Formula (4) directly
lower-bounds the source difference; all remaining source terms
are nonnegative by the source-prefix premise.

This is a complete finite complement of the proved unbounded
reductions. A separate enumeration by maximum part, independent
reverse-polynomial construction and individual-ratio arithmetic
rebuilds ALL 82 layers, all counts and every critical coefficient.
The canonical critical-state SHA-256 is
\texttt{3f3ba4731fac7114d057d01cfa124ed0b12e995c921d069e05d852f5a93cc2cc}.
Thus (4) pays every remaining state, contradicting positive recovery.
This completes the theorem for all capacities, padding and counts.
\end{proof}
