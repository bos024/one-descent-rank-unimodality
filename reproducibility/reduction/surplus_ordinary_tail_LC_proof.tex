\section{A full ordinary coefficient LC tail in the binary-surplus family}
For every actual normalized sorted arm $1^\alpha2^uV$ with nonempty
nonbinary V, put $r=|V|$, $h=\sum(t-1)$, $W=h+r$, $w=u+r$.
We prove, at ALL capacities/counts/paddings,
\[
 u\ge\max(h,16)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 F_N^2\ge F_{N-1}F_{N+1}\quad(N\ge w+1).             \tag{1}
\]
This is ordinary coefficient LC on a specified tail. It does not
assert global coefficient LC, relative LC or original one-descent UI.

We use the registered entire ordinary quotient theorem
$J=((1+z)^uL-c)/(1+qz)>0$, globally LC, at $u\ge h$ and $u\ge16$,
where $L=\prod[t]$, $q=\phi-1$, $c=\psi^uL(-\phi)$.
The degree-boundary theorem includes its single-interval case;
its primitive's one small exception is immaterial here.
For $u>h$ use the earlier surplus theorem. Only ordinary LC of J,
rather than LC of its positive primitive, is needed below.
We also use ordinary actual tail LC after its first ordinary mode
and the registered nonincreasing finite-initial-padding ordinary
density through that mode.

\paragraph{A cumulative quotient with a paid initial atom.}
Let $E=(1+z)^uL$, $P=\prod t$, $a=\alpha-1$, and
$C=L\sum_{k=0}^{u-1}(z+z^2)^k$.
Let I denote infinite INITIAL padding at fixed u.
At every physical rank used below ($N\ge W$),
\[
 I_N=P+\sum_{i\le N-r-2}C_i,\qquad
 F_{w+2+s}=I_{w+2+s}-\sum_{i\le s-a}E_i.              \tag{2}
\]
The first identity is needed at $s\ge-1$, and holds there since u>=h.
Set $x_s=I_{w+2+s}$ and
\[
 B_s=\phi x_{s-1}-x_s\quad(s\ge0),\qquad B_s=0\quad(s<0).
\]
Exact division and the physical numerator units give
\[
 B_s=qc+\sum_{j=0}^sJ_j,\quad B_0=1-q^2c,\quad
 B_s-B_{s-1}=J_s\ (s\ge1),                          \tag{3}
\]
\[
 \sum_{i\le s}E_i=B_s+qB_{s-1}+q^2c\,\mathbf1_{s=0}.
                                                                  \tag{4}
\]
The special rank-zero term in (4) is retained.

The CI norm gives $|c|\le1$. Write $E_2=\binom w2+r\le w^2/2$.
Direct coefficient identities are
\[
 J_1=w-q(1-c),\quad J_2=E_2-qJ_1,\quad B_1=B_0+J_1.
\]
They imply
\[
 0<B_0\le7/5,\quad w\le B_1\le w+1,\quad
 J_1\ge w-5/4,\quad J_2\le w^2/2.
\]
Thus
\[
 B_1^2-B_0B_2=B_1J_1-B_0J_2
 \ge (3/10)w^2-(5/4)w>0\quad(w\ge6).                \tag{5}
\]
All parameters here are unbounded.

This SINGLE cumulative seed propagates through the entire LC J tail.
For positive consecutive primitive values p,q,r and cumulative values
A,B=A+p,C=B+q, the exact identity is
\[
 p(Cq-Br)=q(Bp-Aq)+B(q^2-pr).
\]
Both right terms are nonnegative whenever the preceding cumulative
seed and primitive LC hold. Full induction proves all subsequent
cumulative LC minors. At the primitive's terminal endpoint and the
constant B tail the conclusion is immediate.
Consequently B is globally LC on nonnegative indices, positive,
nondecreasing, and eventually constant. Its positive primitive after
deleting rank zero is exactly the globally LC rank-one truncation of J.

\paragraph{A bounded future inverse and a legitimate infinite cut.}
The bounded recurrence $x_{s-1}=q(x_s+B_s)$ gives
\[
                    x_s=\sum_{j\ge1}q^jB_{s+j}\quad(s\ge-1).       \tag{6}
\]
The terminal constant in I fixes the bounded solution uniquely.
For earlier negative indices the zero-extended B inverse need not
equal actual I; those indices are not silently substituted.
The LC future-geometric convolution of B is LC.
One may prove this using finite truncations and pass to the limit:
B is bounded, so geometric domination gives convergence at each
of the three read coefficients. Finite truncation at the right endpoint
preserves LC, as does truncation of the geometric kernel.
Thus x is LC at every centre $s\ge0$ used in (2).

Apply the registered signed-primitive geometric-cut theorem to B.
Although B has an infinite constant tail, truncate it at R beyond the
beginning M of its first maximal plateau and beyond the finite read band.
The finite truncation is positive UI/LC, has the SAME first mode M,
and has the SAME positive primitive after deleting rank zero.
Its final drop is negative and hence does not enter that primitive.
For any fixed read the finite cut theorem is therefore valid at
$a+2\le s<M$. Letting R tend to infinity preserves each LC minor
and positive part, yielding the same conclusion for (6).
By (4), the true cumulative-E source differs from $B+qzB$ ONLY at
index zero; the triples agree at every centre $s\ge a+2$.
No constant spectral atom is erased.

The infinite-initial-padding ordinary sequence x is constant from
its first mode onward. Its exact ordinary density for actual finite
padding is nonincreasing THROUGH the actual first ordinary mode.
A strict actual increase beyond the first constant x rank would
contradict that density order. Hence the actual first ordinary mode
has offset at most M. At a global ordinary mode LC is automatic.
The cut theorem covers all remaining pre-mode centres after startup;
the established ordinary tail-LC theorem covers all post-mode centres.

\paragraph{All startup and first-defect boundaries.}
Put $M_0=1$, $M_1=w+1$, $M_2=E_2+w+1$, the first THREE coefficients
of the TRUE cumulative E source. CI coefficients give, at w>=6,
\[
 M_2\ge(w^2+w+4)/2>2\phi(w+1),\qquad M_2\le(w+1)^2.   \tag{7}
\]
Before s=a the source is absent. At a-1 only the next unit is removed,
improving the unperturbed minor whenever that centre is at least zero.
At a the new signed minor is
\[
 M_x-2x_a+1+x_{a-1}M_1
 \ge M_x+x_{a-1}(w+1-2\phi)+1>0.
\]
At a+1 it is
\[
 M_x+x_{a-1}M_2-2M_1x_a+x_{a+1}+M_1^2-M_2>0
\]
by (7) and $x_a<\phi x_{a-1}$.
The latter ordinary golden ceiling is registered at EVERY
physical $N\ge\max(W,w+2)=w+2$, precisely s>=0.
If a startup centre is already past the actual mode, ordinary tail LC
applies instead. At centres before the actual mode the actual support
and positivity guards are automatic. These payments retain every
finite initial-padding term.

There remains the FIRST centre N=w+1 (s=-1), whose earlier neighbor
is not read from the zero-extended B inverse.
Let R be the infinite-BINARY reference. Actual coefficients through
w+1 agree with R, and only the NEXT coefficient loses a positive
integer delta=1 or2. Let A>0 be the reference's fixed homogeneous
Cassini amplitude. Its current ordinary minor is at least -A,
so the actual first-boundary minor is at least
\[
                          -A+\delta R_w\ge0.          \tag{8}
\]
Here $w\ge W$ and R increases; it suffices that $R_W\ge A$.
For two or more factors, write x=Dref_W,y=Dref_(W+1).
The exact reference norm gives
\[
 A=x^2+xy-y^2=\phi\sqrt5\,\rho x-\phi^2\rho^2,\quad
 \rho\le(2q^2)^r\le16/25.
\]
Using $\phi\le13/8$, $\sqrt5\le9/4$ and the registered
$y\ge3x/2$ gives $A\le117x/50<5x/2\le x+y=R_W$.
For one factor L, the exact Fibonacci identities instead give
\[
 R_W-A=f_{L-2}-(-1)^{L+1}\ge0\quad(L\ge3).
\]
This includes L=3 with equality. Thus (8) covers every capacity list.
When a=0 it also pays the startup centre a-1; its extra source unit
only improves the boundary. This completes (1).
