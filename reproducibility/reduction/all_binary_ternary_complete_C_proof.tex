\section{Complete actual C for every binary--ternary arm}
All arms in this section are actual normalized sorted arms
$X=1^\alpha2^u3^r$, with $\alpha\ge1$, $u,r\ge0$.
Use the ordinary coefficient sequence F, its positive differences
$D_N=(F_N-F_{N-1})^+$ for N>=2, $D_0=D_1=0$, and
$G_k=\sum_{i=0}^kF_i$. Full C means
\[
             G_kD_{N-1}\ge G_{k-1}D_N
                      \quad(N\ge4,\ 1\le k\le N-3).
\]

\begin{theorem}[All binary and ternary counts]
EVERY such X has full C at every current rank and every shift at
least three. Both counts, padding, current rank and shift are
unbounded. No binary-count cutoff remains in this alphabet.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp ordinary coefficient classification]
For EVERY r>=6,u>=1 and EVERY alpha>=1, the entire ordinary
coefficient tail from centre r+3 is LC if and only if u<=2r.
For every u>=2r+1 its ACTUAL minor at centre 3r+1 is exactly
\[
                   F_{3r+1}^2-F_{3r}F_{3r+2}=-4^r<0.
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
This failure does not contradict full C or ordinary unimodality.
The original general one-descent coefficient-unimodality conjecture,
general C for arbitrary capacities, and full A2 remain OPEN.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Previously proved resources, used with their exact scopes.}
Write $S=z+z^2+z^3$, $T=z+z^2$, $B_r=\sum_{j<r}S^j$,
$H_2=(1-T)^{-1}$, and
$R^{(r)}=B_r+S^rH_2$. Put W=3r and A=r+3.
We use the new stationary-reference theorem: R has full C for
every r, and, for r>=6, its ordinary coefficients are LC at
every centre A<=n<=W. Ordinary reference LC is not asserted after W.
We also use the OLD universal ordinary-tail theorem: if u>=5 and
$h=2r>u$, actual F is LC at EVERY centre n>=W-1. This is the
sharp one-earlier theorem, not an assumption about the present
early band. At u=h, the OLD surplus refinement gives ordinary
F LC at every centre n>=u+r+1, since u+r>=6.

The OLD actual structure also gives ordinary F LC at every centre
on and after its FIRST D mode L. Before the first F mode this is
concavity from nonincreasing nonnegative slopes; afterwards it is
the established decreasing-side ordinary LC. Thus a proof of
ordinary LC at A<=n<L supplies the entire tail from A.
Finally the proved ordinary-to-C bridge says that such a tail
implies full C for every mixed arm u>=1: its first ordinary loss
is at A, its entry ratio is below the tribonacci root, and its
protected reference G ratios are above that root.

\paragraph{Uniform ordinary-tail heredity for ANY binary count.}
For every fixed u>=1, alpha>=1, and old r>=6, ordinary F LC
on the entire tail n>=r+3 implies the same assertion at r+1.
This removes the earlier restriction u>=4 from the scalar source
argument; no new uniform head-equality assumption is inserted.

Let $f=F_{1^\alpha2^u}$. Exactly
\[
 H_2-f=T^u\{H_2-[\alpha+1]\}\ge0.
\]
For u>=1 the coefficients through degree two agree with H2 and H3.
Define the first three ordinary losses of f relative to H3 by
a,b,c at degrees 3,4,5. They need not be fixed as alpha varies.
Coefficientwise domination by H2 gives the uniform bounds
\[
                             a\ge1,\quad b\ge2,\quad c\ge5.
                                                               \tag{2}
\]
After appending r threes, the first three ordinary losses relative
to H3, at r+3,r+4,r+5, are EXACTLY
\[
                a,\quad B=ar+b,\quad C=ar(r+1)/2+br+c.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
Every finite-padding correction is contained in a,b,c. None is
discarded or replaced by a large-padding model.

For a new count r>=7, use the ordinary H3 minor bound
$M_h(n)\ge-h_{n-1}$ and $h_n\le2h_{n-1}$. At its first
new centre the minor is bounded below by
$h_{n-1}(B-4a-1)+a^2\ge0$. At the next centre, writing
U,V,W for the reference triple, its exact expansion is
\[
 (V-B)^2-(U-a)(W-C)
   =M_h-2BV+B^2+UC+a(W-C)
   \ge U(C-4B-1)+B^2+a(W-C)\ge0.
\]
Here W-C is the ACTUAL next ordinary coefficient, hence nonnegative;
and (2) gives
\[
 B-4a-1=a(r-4)+b-1\ge0,\qquad
 C-4B-1=ar(r-7)/2+b(r-4)+c-1\ge0.               \tag{4}
\]
One generic real Lean theorem checks both complete payments on
the unbounded domain (2), r>=7.

Every later centre inherits by ordinary LC convolution. Indeed
$F_{r+1}=1+S F_r$; truncate old F below r+2. The truncated
sequence is globally LC under the old tail hypothesis. Its S
convolution agrees on the entire new three-read triple for every
n>=r+6. The new two preceding centres r+4,r+5 were just paid
by (3),(4), and the unit at rank zero is absent. This proves
the whole r-heredity, including later modes and terminal zeros.

Uniform r-heredity now proves the ordinary tail for every

There is no unbounded diagonal premise left to check.

\paragraph{The boundary u=2r and the exact sharp complement.}
At u=2r,r>=6, the actual sequence first differs from its reference
at W+2. Its ordinary triples through centre W are protected and
paid by reference LC. The OLD surplus ordinary-tail refinement
starts at u+r+1=W+1, so it supplies every remaining centre.
This closes the equality case u=2r.

For a precise counterfamily above that boundary, let
$C_r=1+z^3B_r$, so R=C_r/(1-z-z^2). Its recurrence is
$C_{r+1}=S C_r+(1-z-z^2)$, $C_0=1$.
Because $S-2z=-z(1-z-z^2)$, full commutative-ring induction gives
\[
             C_r=(2z)^r+(1-z-z^2)J_r,
\quad J_0=0,\quad J_{r+1}=1+S J_r-z(2z)^r.
\]
For r>=1, deg J_r<=3r-2 by this recurrence. Thus, with fib0=0,
fib1=1,
\[
             R_n=2^r\operatorname{fib}(n-r+1)
                                  \quad(n\ge3r-1).       \tag{5}
\]
If u>=2r+1, the first actual loss is at u+r+2>=3r+3.
The triple at centre 3r+1 therefore equals the reference triple
\[
 2^r\bigl(\operatorname{fib}(2r+1),
          \operatorname{fib}(2r+2),
          \operatorname{fib}(2r+3)\bigr).
\]
Even-index Cassini gives (1). The whole polynomial recurrence
identity and the complete unbounded even Cassini induction are
separate Lean reports. This supplies an exact, arbitrary-padding,
unbounded negative complement and proves the stated iff.
