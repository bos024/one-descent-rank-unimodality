\section{A sharper single-arm primitive envelope omits the initial interval}
For $Z=1^\alpha U$, put $a=\operatorname{wt}Z$, $h=|U|$,
$D=\sum_{t\in U}(t-1)=a-\alpha-h$, $K_Z=\prod_{t\in U}[t]$, and
$d=(\Delta q_Z)^+$. Then the ENTIRE relative density
\[
                              d_n/(K_Z)_n
\]
is nonincreasing, in the cross-multiplied sense.

This strengthens the existing single-arm main-box envelope by
omitting $[\alpha+1]$. The proof is the SAME generic interval-window
positive/negative-difference transport from
\texttt{ordinary\_relative\_growth.tex}, with the sharper initial
pair $d=1$, $K=1$ for an all-ones arm. Under appending $t$ the
positive differences are those of $[t]q$, since the final unit
creates no positive difference. The denominator becomes $[t]K$.
The already kernel-checked generic transport proves the full induction.
This sharpening is asserted for a SINGLE arm; its naive two-arm
counterpart without the longer initial interval is not asserted.

At a strictly increasing primitive edge $d_{r+1}>d_r>0$, the
proved skeleton-center theorem gives $2r+2\le D$. The CI degree
rank-ratio bound and this sharper relative order imply
\[
 (r+1)(d_{r+1}-d_r)\le(D-2r-1)d_r.                    \tag{1}
\]
Indeed the skeleton box $K$ satisfies
$(r+1)K_{r+1}\le(D-r)K_r$ on this side of its center, and
$d_{r+1}K_r\le d_rK_{r+1}$. Its positive coefficient $K_r$
can be cancelled. The unbounded scalar implication is kernel checked.

\section{The first opposite source pays a sharp gap of five}
\begin{theorem}[Unconditional mixed-tail nonrecovery from the source birth]
Let $Z=1^\alpha U$, $Y$ be actual increasing old arms, each with
at least two non-one parts. At $j\ge2$, assume $F_Z(j+1)\le F_Z(j)$,
and that the ENTIRE prefix of $E_Y$ through $j+1$ is nondecreasing.
The latter holds, in particular, whenever $F_Y$ has not yet passed
its global plateau, by the proved forward-slope doubling theorem.
Write $\kappa=\kappa_Y$. If
\[
                              \alpha\ge\kappa-5,       \tag{2}
\]
then, for $p=q_Zq_Y$, $n=a+b-j$ and $e=(2-z)p$,
\[
                               e_{n+1}\le e_n.         \tag{3}
\]
Neither C, a half-weight assumption, nonnegative $e_n$, nor OLD A2
is required for (3). With the usual nonnegative head input from
OLD A2, it closes S at EVERY head in this branch.
The constant five is optimal for this uniform pointwise conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $A=F_Z$, $Q=E_Y A$, $r=a-j$, and $s_r=\Delta q_Z(r)$.
The exact source identities give
\[
 e_n=Q_{j+1}+s_r,\quad e_{n+1}=Q_j+s_{r+1},\quad
 T=e_{n+1}-e_n=(s_{r+1}-s_r)-(Q_{j+1}-Q_j).          \tag{4}
\]
The source-prefix assumption gives
$w_i=\Delta E_Y(i)\ge0$ through $j+1$, while $w_\kappa\ge1$.
Thus $Q$ is nondecreasing there, and, whenever $\kappa\le j+1$,
\[
             Q_{j+1}-Q_j=\sum_i w_i A_{j+1-i}
                                      \ge A_{j+1-\kappa}.       \tag{5}
\]
This is a direct positive convolution term; no coefficient bound
has been differentiated. The finite-sum birth payment is kernel checked.

For $r\le-2$, the primitive contribution in (4) is zero, so $T\le0$.
For $r=-1$ it is one. Here $j=a+1$, and, writing
$\delta=\kappa-\alpha\le5$, the index in (5) is
$D+h+2-\delta\ge1$, since $D\ge h\ge2$.
It is at most $a-1$ because $\kappa\ge3$.
Every actual ordinary coefficient on its support is at least one,
so (5) pays this case as well.

Let $r\ge0$ and suppose for contradiction $T>0$.
Then $s_r\ge0$ by the nonincreasing $A$ premise, and (4) gives
$s_{r+1}>s_r$. Initial interval support of the reverse positive
differences excludes $s_r=0$, so
$d_r=s_r>0$, $d_{r+1}=s_{r+1}>d_r$.
Put $e_0=D-2r-2\ge0$. The positive curvature to be paid is
$C=d_{r+1}-d_r$, and (1) becomes
\[
                         (r+1)C\le(e_0+1)d_r.         \tag{6}
\]
The birth index is
\[
 j+1-\kappa=D-r+h+1-\delta\ge r\ge0,
\]
so (5) is indeed present. In reverse coordinates it is
$q_Z(v)$ with $v=r+\kappa-1$.

Every ordinary $q_Z$ is a sum of shifted CI boxes with endpoint
sums at least $M=a-h=\alpha+D$. Consequently
\[
          q_Z(v)\ge q_Z(u)\quad\hbox{if }u\le v,\quad u+v\le M.
                                                               \tag{7}
\]
This is the established lower-center reflection, applied separately
to the positive boxes; it does not require full LC of $q_Z$.
If $2r+\kappa\le M$, use $u=r+1$ in (7).
It pays $C\le q_Z(r+1)$ immediately.

Otherwise put $s=2r+\kappa-M=\delta-e_0-2>0$.
Condition (2) leaves precisely
\[
 1\le s\le3,\quad0\le e_0\le2,\quad3\le\delta\le5.
\]
Use $u=r-s+1$ in (7); its pair with $v$ has endpoint sum EXACTLY $M$.
For $r\ge10$ the following three complete estimates pay (6):
\begin{itemize}
\item $s=1$: $e_0\le2$ gives $C\le3d_r/(r+1)\le d_r\le q_Z(r)$.
\item $s=2$: $e_0\le1$ and $h\le D\le2r+3$ give, by the ordinary
primitive Hilbert growth,
$d_r\le(3r+2)d_{r-1}/r$.
Thus $C\le2(3r+2)d_{r-1}/(r(r+1))\le d_{r-1}\le q_Z(r-1)$.
\item $s=3$: necessarily $e_0=0$, $h\le2r+2$.
Two Hilbert steps give
$(r-1)d_r\le3(3r+1)d_{r-2}$.
Hence $C\le3(3r+1)d_{r-2}/((r+1)(r-1))\le d_{r-2}\le q_Z(r-2)$.
\end{itemize}
All denominator and polynomial comparisons for $r\ge10$ are kernel
checked. Together with (5)--(7), each gives $T\le0$, a contradiction.

The ENTIRE remaining complement has $0\le r\le9$,
$e_0\in\{0,1,2\}$, $D=2r+2+e_0$, and
$e_0+3\le\delta\le5$.
Enumerate all partitions of $D$ into $h\ge2$ positive increments
$t_i-1$. This covers ALL sorted owner capacities and every possible
count, without a hidden host-size bound.
For each owner let $b_0=D-r+h+1-\delta$ and
$N=\max(b_0,r+1)$. Only $q_Z$ through rank $r+1$ and $A_{b_0}$
are needed. All $\alpha\ge N$ agree on both: the initial interval
and every reverse terminal source have identical low coefficients
after clipping $\alpha$ to $N$. Thus $1\le\alpha\le N$ covers
ALL initial runs exactly.

There are 154048 complete representatives, of which 96070 have
the required strict positive primitive curvature. Every one satisfies
\[
                           A_{b_0}\ge C.              \tag{8}
\]
An independent full-forward recurrence and separate partitions-of-$D$
enumeration reconstruct every state; all ten canonical layer hashes,
counts and six equality cases agree. This is an exhaustive finite
complement of the proved unbounded reductions, not a random search.
Equation (8) pays (5), contradicting $T>0$. This proves (3).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Sharpness and consequences for the remaining research}
For every $\alpha\ge1$, take
\[
 Z=1^\alpha2^2,\quad a=\alpha+4,\quad
 Y=1(\alpha+5)^{\alpha+4},\quad j=a.
\]

$\kappa_Y=\alpha+6$. Its compared tail is
$e_n=d_0=1$, $e_{n+1}=d_1=2$, so it recovers by one.
Thus replacing five by six in (2) is FALSE, on an unbounded
actual family. This is not an S or original-UI counterexample:

original family is independently proved. All usual geometric S

$P_{a-1}\ge2^{\alpha+3}>\alpha+8=p_1$ and $P_a>1$.

on the opposite's FIRST MISSING weight, not on its maximum capacity;
its length and maximum capacity can be unbounded.

This is a relative source/first-capacity constraint; no original
