\section{Complete late C for every finite tail of the infinite binary reference}
Put $H=1/(1-z-z^2)$. Starting from $F_\varnothing=H$, append ANY
finite capacity word $V$ with entries at least two by
$F_{Vt}=1+S_tF_V$, $S_t=z+\cdots+z^t$.
Its order need not be sorted. Set
\[
 B_V=(1-z-z^2)F_V,\qquad W=\deg B_V,\quad
 d=(1-z)F_V,\quad G=F_V/(1-z),\quad G_{-1}=0.
\]
For $B_V=1$ take $W=0$.

\begin{theorem}[Every word, every late rank, every C shift]
For every such word and every $N\ge W+6$, ALL shifts $R\ge3$ satisfy
\[
                 G_{N-R}d_{N-1}\ge G_{N-R-1}d_N.           \tag{1}
\]
Negative-index cumulative coefficients are zero. When $3\le R\le N$,
the quantitative lower bound is
\[
 G_{N-R}d_{N-1}-G_{N-R-1}d_N
    \ge\left(\frac13-2\varphi^{-4}\right)d_{N-1}>0,
 \qquad \varphi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}.                        \tag{2}
\]
Here $W$ equals the sum of capacities after deleting all LEADING twos
from $V$. In particular no count, capacity or padding cutoff is imposed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $S_2=z+z^2$. The already established numerator-cascade identity,
specialized to the fixed binary reference, is
\[
 B_\varnothing=1,\qquad
 B_{Vt}=1+S_t(B_V-1)+(S_t-S_2).                            \tag{3}
\]
It proves by full word induction that $B_V\ge0$, $B_0=1$,
$B_1=B_2=0$, and degree at most the word weight. A leading two
fixes $H$, so it can be deleted. Once a capacity above two first
appears, (3) has positive leading coefficient at that capacity;
every subsequent append increases the degree by exactly its capacity.
This proves the assertion about $W$.
The cascade identity is an old input from
\texttt{count\_prefix\_and\_stationary\_resource\_separator.tex};
the spectral/cumulative payment below is the new argument.

Let $\psi=-\varphi^{-1}=1-\varphi$ and
$g_k=\operatorname{Fib}(k+3)-1$, $g_{-1}=0$.
These are precisely the coefficients of $H/(1-z)$.
At zero, $g_0-\varphi g_{-1}=1$; for $k\ge1$, Binet's
identity gives
\[
 g_k-\varphi g_{k-1}=\varphi-1+\psi^{k+2}
                                  \ge2-\varphi>\tfrac13. \tag{4}
\]
Indeed $|\psi|<1$ and the most negative term in this range is
$\psi^3$ at $k=1$; $\varphi-1+\psi^3=2-\varphi$.
The uniform $1/3$ lower bound is also kernel proved directly with
exact rational bounds on $\varphi,\psi$, without floating arithmetic.

Since $G=(H/(1-z))B$, exact finite convolution gives
\[
 G_k-\varphi G_{k-1}
    =\sum_i B_i\{g_{k-i}-\varphi g_{k-i-1}\}\ge\tfrac13
                                               \quad(k\ge0). \tag{5}
\]
Negative base indices contribute zero; all remaining terms are
nonnegative and the term $i=0$ alone pays (5).
This transports a DIFFERENCE identity, rather than differentiating
a coefficientwise majorant.

At the conjugate reciprocal root $\psi^{-1}=-\varphi$,
the constant correction $1-S_2$ vanishes. Consequently
\[
                B_{Vt}(-\varphi)=S_t(-\varphi)B_V(-\varphi).
\]
For EVERY capacity $t\ge2$,
\[
 \varphi S_t(-\varphi)=(-\varphi)^t-1,\qquad
                         |S_t(-\varphi)|\le\varphi^t.     \tag{6}
\]
The second inequality follows from the first, the triangle inequality,
and $1+\varphi^t\le\varphi^{t+1}$.
Multiplying (6) over the word after its leading twos have been removed,
\[
                              |B_V(-\varphi)|\le\varphi^W. \tag{7}
\]

For $N\ge W+2$, the whole reference slope is a finite convolution
of $B$ with the binary slopes. All its read indices are at least two,
so the exact Fibonacci identity yields
\[
 \epsilon_N:=d_N-\varphi d_{N-1}
       =\sum_i B_i\psi^{N-i-2}
       =\psi^{N-2}B_V(\psi^{-1}).                         \tag{8}
\]
Equivalently,
$\varphi^{N-2}\epsilon_N=(-1)^{N-2}B_V(-\varphi)$.
No estimate is made before the finite tail has entirely entered this
homogeneous range. Combining (7)--(8), for $N\ge W+6$,
\[
                       |\epsilon_N|\le\varphi^{-4}<\tfrac16. \tag{9}
\]
The strict inequality uses $\varphi^4=3\varphi+2>6$.

The coefficients of $F=HB$ are nondecreasing, since $H$ is
nondecreasing and $B\ge0$. Put $c_B=B(1)\ge1$.
For $m\ge W$, the exact binary cumulative identity gives
\[
                         G_m=F_{m+2}-c_B.                 \tag{10}
\]
Also $F$ satisfies its Fibonacci recurrence beyond degree $B$.
At the present ranks this implies
\[
 G_{N-4}=F_{N-2}-c_B\le2F_{N-3}=2d_{N-1}.                \tag{11}
\]
All the inequalities in (11) use the entire homogeneous tail,
not the prefix where numerator births can still occur.

Fix $R\ge3$ and put $k=N-R$. If $k<0$, both terms of (1)
vanish. Otherwise $G_{k-1}\ge0$ and
$G_{k-1}\le G_{N-4}\le2d_{N-1}$, with $G_{-1}=0$ when $k=0$.
Use (5), (8), and (9) in the exact minor:
\[
 \begin{split}
 G_kd_{N-1}-G_{k-1}d_N
 &=d_{N-1}(G_k-\varphi G_{k-1})-G_{k-1}\epsilon_N\\
 &\ge \tfrac13d_{N-1}-2d_{N-1}|\epsilon_N|\\
 &\ge(\tfrac13-2\varphi^{-4})d_{N-1}>0.
 \end{split}
\]
The reference slopes are strictly positive from rank two, so this
proves (1)--(2) for every required shift.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
The uniform cumulative golden-ratio margin (5) holds at ALL ranks
for every reference word. The C conclusion covers ALL shifts at
EVERY current rank $N\ge\deg B+6$.
It does not assert earlier multiple-tail reference C at
$N\le\deg B+5$, global cumulative LC of every reference, or C for
arbitrary finite actual binary prefixes. Normalized-relative or
read-prefix inheritance still requires its proved density hypotheses;
those hypotheses are not silently supplied by this reference theorem.
General actual C/A2 and the original general unamplified conjecture
remain open. No original least-counterexample bound is upgraded.
