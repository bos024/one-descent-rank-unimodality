\section{A sharp one-earlier ordinary coefficient LC tail}

$h=\sum(t-1)$, $W=\sum V$, and assume
\[

\]
Then the ENTIRE ordinary coefficient tail is LC from centre W-1:
\[
                         F_N^2\ge F_{N-1}F_{N+1}

\]

actual coefficient triples and arbitrary finite initial padding.

\paragraph{Binary cumulatives and the one-factor crossing.}

cumulative sequence G. Its ordinary coefficients are the Fibonacci

\[
                         g_n^2-g_{n-1}g_{n+1}
                              =f_n+(-1)^{n+2}\ge0.
\]
At n=1 it is zero; at n>=2 use $f_n\ge1$.
Thus the actual cumulative prefix through centre u+1 is LC, including
the first-defect boundary. The established ENTIRE ordinary binary
LC tail begins at u+1. Ordinary ratio order and the paid cumulative
seed then integrate every later cumulative LC comparison.
At the finite right endpoint G becomes constant, which is harmless.
This is an unbounded proof, with no finite padding cutoff.

If the first nonbinary crossing is a single capacity L, then
$L-1>u$ and, at every $1\le n\le L$,
$F_{1^\alpha2^uL}(n)=G_{1^\alpha2^u}(n-1)$.
