\section{Actual padding-three late C from a local simultaneous prefix}
For EVERY nonempty sorted CI tail $V$ of degree $h$, length $r$,
weight $W=h+r$, and EVERY binary count $u\ge h+1$, the actual
arm $1^3 2^uV$ has LC positive slopes at all moving centres
$u+r+1\le N<\ell$, where $\ell$ is its first global positive-slope
mode. Every actual C shift $R\ge3$ holds at EVERY $N\ge W+1$.
The theorem does not assert earlier reference C or smaller counts.

\begin{proof}
Use the fixed-padding count induction to reduce to $u=h+1$;
put $m=h$, $C^3=C^\infty-z^{m+3}(1+z)^uL$, $A=(C^3)^+$.
Before the subtraction starts the Fibonacci prefix strictly grows,
and $A_m>A_{m-1}$ with all earlier formal values smaller than $A_m$.
The exact actual signed-tail identity holds from $n=h-1$ onward.
The actual positive-slope unimodality therefore identifies its first
mode with the first global mode $e$ of $A$ in this tail.

Put $P=1+z+2z^2+z^3$, $c=q^2\psi^uL(-\phi)$, and
$J=\{P(1+z)^uL-c\}/(1+qz)$.
The exact identities $P(-\phi)=q^2$ and
$(1-z)P=1+z^2(1-z-z^2)$ give
\[
       \phi C^3_{u+s-1}-C^3_{u+s}=[z^s](1-z)J
                                      \quad(1\le s\le u).
\]
The zero-index primitive is not used. The separately proved local
prefix theorem supplies positive-primitive LC through centre $u-2$;
no global ordinary LC of this quotient is silently assumed.
The likelihood-ratio source comparison gives positive deficits on
the positive actual tail and $e\le\lceil(3m+h)/2\rceil=2m$.
Thus all needed deficit triples at centres $m+3,\ldots,e$ read
primitive centres $2,\ldots,u-2$ and neighbours $1,\ldots,u$.
Clipping the last next coefficient only lowers the next deficit.
Backward geometric-deficit integration from $e$ gives coefficient
LC through centre $m+2$. The already proved strict actual seeds
at $m,m+1$, with first defect one and second defect $W+2$ at
padding three, finish the entrance.

Propagate at this SAME fixed padding by the existing count induction
to every $u\ge h+1$. The homogeneous reference C theorem from $W+1$
and the TWO ordinary-F/read seeds then pay all ranks and shifts
in the stated late range by the proved D/F/G integration and join.
\end{proof}

At the single entrance the residual short paddings are now
$4\le\alpha\le h$. The companion prefix theorem and its complete
small exceptions have passed formal scalar and independent arithmetic
audits. This is not
general C/A2 or original unamplified unimodality, and gives no
numerical least-counterexample bound for that original conjecture.
