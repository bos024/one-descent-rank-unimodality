\paragraph{Normalized geometric tilts preserve additive cumulative margins.}
Use the existing atom models
\[
 Q_D=1+\sum_{n\ge2}D_nz^n,\qquad
 F_D=Q_D/(1-z),\quad G_D=Q_D/(1-z)^2,
 \qquad D^a_n=a^{n-2}D_n,\quad0<a\le1.
\]
Suppose $H_i=G_D(i)-\lambda G_D(i-1)\ge c$ for $1\le i\le k$,
where $0\le c\le1$, $H_0=1$, and $\mu=a\lambda\ge1$.
The exact registered coefficient identity is
\[
 a^2\{G_{D^a}(k)-\mu G_{D^a}(k-1)\}
 =\sum_{i=0}^k K_a(k-i)a^iH_i
       +(a^2-1)\{1+k(1-\mu)\},                       \tag{1}
\]
where $K_a(0)=1$ and
$K_a(j)=2(1-a)+(j-1)(1-a)^2$ for $j\ge1$.
All weights in the sum are nonnegative. Its COMPLETE row sum is
\[
 \sum_{i=0}^kK_a(k-i)a^i=1+k(1-a).
\]
One unbounded induction proves this identity: the row totals start at1
and satisfy $T_{k+1}=aT_k+2(1-a)+k(1-a)^2$.
Keeping the rank-zero term one, rather than replacing it by the
weaker bound c, bounds the right side of (1) below by
\[
 K_a(k)+c\{1+k(1-a)-K_a(k)\}
       +(a^2-1)\{1+k(1-\mu)\}.
\]
Its difference from $a^2c$ is EXACTLY
\[
 k(1-a)\{(1-c)(1-a)+c+(1+a)(\mu-1)\}\ge0.           \tag{2}
\]
Hence every needed tilted cumulative margin is at least c.
No division at $a=0$ is used; actual tilts have $a>0$.
The unit atom is essential.

\paragraph{An all-capacity ordinary seed for the tilted one-jump reference.}
Let $f_n=\operatorname{Fib}(n)$, $f_0=0$, $f_1=1$.
The reference $R=1+S_L/(1-z-z^2)$ has
$r_n=f_n-f_{n-L}$ for $n\ge2$.
In particular $r_L=f_L$, $r_{L+1}=f_{L+1}-1$.
For $n\le L$ its normalized tilt has ordinary values
\[
 T_n=1+\sum_{i=2}^n a^{i-2}f_i,\quad T_0=T_1=1.
\]
Put $\mu=a(f_{L+1}-1)/f_L$. We prove the UNIFORM polynomial bound
\[
 P_L(a):=f_LT_L-a(f_{L+1}-1)T_{L-1}\ge4
                   \qquad(L\ge3,\ 0\le a\le1).       \tag{3}
\]
At L=3 the polynomial is exactly4. For L>=4, collecting EVERY
coefficient gives
\[
\begin{split}
 P_L(a)={}&2f_L+(2-2f_{L-1})a\\
 &+\sum_{k=2}^{L-3}
 \{(-1)^{k+1}f_{L-k-1}+f_{k+1}\}a^k\\
 &+\{f_{L-1}+(-1)^{L+1}\}a^{L-2}.                  \tag{4}
\end{split}
\]
The middle coefficient identity is the full Fibonacci determinant
$f_Lf_{k+2}-f_{L+1}f_{k+1}=(-1)^{k+1}f_{L-k-1}$.
The last coefficient is Cassini. These are exact identities at
arbitrary indices, not sampled polynomial interpolation.

The last term of (4) is nonnegative. Its first linear coefficient
is nonpositive and therefore its contribution is at least
$2-2f_{L-1}$. Each middle contribution is at least $-f_{L-k-1}$,
since $0\le a^k\le1$. The ENTIRE negative budget is
\[
 \sum_{k=2}^{L-3}f_{L-k-1}
       =\sum_{j=2}^{L-3}f_j=f_{L-1}-2.
\]
It follows that
\[
 P_L(a)\ge2f_L-3f_{L-1}+4=f_{L-4}+4\ge4.
\]
The empty middle sum at L=4 is included. The complete finite-sum
payment and both unbounded Fibonacci identities are kernel checked.
In particular (3) proves the ordinary tilted seed
$T_L-\mu T_{L-1}>0$ whenever $a>0$.
