\section{Full property C through five non-one parts}
\begin{theorem}
Every actual normalized increasing arm with at most five non-one parts
has full property C at every rank and shift. The capacities and initial-one
length are unbounded. Its ordinary product with any opposite increasing
arm has unimodal factor-two coefficient tilt.
\end{theorem}

Let $X=1^\alpha U$ be an actual increasing normalized arm, $\alpha\ge1$,
$U_i\ge2$. Write $F_X$ for its forward rank polynomial, $G_X$ for
its cumulative coefficients, and $D_0=D_1=0$,
$D_n=(F_X(n)-F_X(n-1))^+$ for $n\ge2$.
Then
\[
 G_X(n+1-r)D_n\ge G_X(n-r)D_{n+1}
       \qquad\text{for every rank $n$ and every $r\ge3$},
                                                               \tag{1}
\]
whenever $|U|\le5$. Every capacity, initial-one length and shift is
unrestricted. Negative-index coefficients are zero.

The proof through four non-one parts is complete. For five parts,
write $U=(a,b,c,d,e)$. The exact initial exchange gives an equivalent
actual representation with $a\le\alpha+1$; it preserves the entire
actual polynomial, old derivative mode and all later capacities.
Its infinite reference changes, so apply all source masks to this
canonical representation itself. Conditional old-C transfer from the
proved four-part theorem leaves only
\[
        n=e+s\le\ell_D(1^\alpha abcd),\qquad
        s\ge5,\qquad3\le r\le s.                         \tag{2}
\]
The sharp center gives $2n\le a+b+c+d+4$.
The complete source geometry, including shorter suffix terms, has
nine masks. Its $bccd$ mask is impossible by actual old-mode geometry;
its $cd$ mask is positive for all finite initial-one lengths by the
completed uniform-boundary theorem. The remaining seven are covered by
the following complete finite-alpha chapters:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{mask}&\text{exact positive regions/cones}&\text{nonzero positive coefficients}\\\hline
\varnothing&96&572771\\
bc&22&16918\\
abbc&51&123900\\
ab&109&1301782\\
abac&209&2526231\\
abacbc&528&10664220\\
abacad&609&14094751.
\end{array}
\]
The earlier reference theorem handles $x=n-\alpha-4\le0$;
the chapters cover all remaining $x\ge1$. The last two use the
new prefix-window/source coupling to eliminate all finite capacity
cuts in the triangle mask and all but the first cut in the star mask.
Their exact closed-cone covers retain boundary faces and prove strict
minor positivity. Both final certificates have passed full independent
coefficient and closed-cover verification. Thus every possible failure
in (2) is excluded. All other
ranks and shifts were already transferred from the four-part theorem,
which completes (1).

The existing single-arm C-to-clipped-R reduction then gives, for ANY
opposite actual increasing arm $Y$, unimodality of the ordinary product's
factor-two coefficient tilt
\[
                         \frac{[z^n]F_XF_Y}{2^n}.
\]
The opposite arm has no restriction on non-one count, capacities or
initial ones. Rectangular cases follow by the existing geometric
uniform-convolution argument.

This is an auxiliary cumulative/ordinary-product theorem. It does NOT
establish the required weighted positive-difference statement A2, the
coupled S induction, or coefficient unimodality of the original
one-descent kernel. The original least-counterexample constraints are
not strengthened merely by moving this auxiliary C frontier from four
to five. General C beyond five, and the original unamplified conjecture,
remain open after this auxiliary theorem.

The full certificates are independently verified exact algebra,
with the previously recorded formal identity proofs for C4 and
finite $bc$, and scoped Lean proofs for generic geometry, mode and
cone-sign arguments. Most new C5 identities are not Lean ring proofs.
No end-to-end composition datatype formalization is claimed.
