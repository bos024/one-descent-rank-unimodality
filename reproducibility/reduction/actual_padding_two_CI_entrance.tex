\section{The actual padding-two entrance at every CI degree}
Let $V$ be a nonempty sorted tail with capacities at least three,
degree $h=\sum_{t\in V}(t-1)$, length $r$, and weight $W=h+r$.
For every binary count $u\ge h+1$, the ACTUAL arm
$1^2 2^u V$ has LC positive slopes at all moving centres
\[
                 u+r+1\le N<\ell,
\]
where $\ell$ is the FIRST global positive-slope mode. Consequently
every actual C comparison holds at EVERY $N\ge W+1$ and EVERY
shift $R\ge3$. Counts, capacities, ranks and shifts are unbounded.
This is a LATE-C theorem. Earlier reference ranks $N\le W$ and
shorter binary counts are not silently covered.

\begin{proof}
It suffices, by the already proved fixed-padding count induction,
to establish the SINGLE entrance $u=h+1$. Put $m=h$ and define
$C^2=C^\infty-z^{m+2}(1+z)^{m+1}L$, $A_n=(C^2_n)^+$.
The exact actual signed tail is $\Delta F(r+2+n)=C^2_n$ for
$n\ge h-1$. Through $n=m=h$ there is no padding-two subtraction,
and the binary Fibonacci prefix convolved with $L$ strictly grows
at every index from two onward. Thus $A_m>A_{m-1}$ and every
earlier formal $A$ value is smaller than $A_m$. The corresponding
ACTUAL edge at physical rank $W+2$ also strictly grows. Actual
positive-slope unimodality therefore puts its first mode in this
exact tail, and identifies it with the first global $A$ mode,
say $e$. The actual pre-mode support is an
interval and the geometric deficits $b_n=\phi A_{n-1}-A_n$
are positive on the positive tail, by the previously proved
likelihood-ratio comparison with $C^\infty$.

Take $U=m+3$ in the surplus-three divided-CI theorem. For
$1\le s\le m+1$ the signed geometric deficit at $n=m+1+s$
is EXACTLY the $s$th primitive coefficient of $J_{U,L}$.
That primitive's positive part is LC. The first-mode bound
$e\le\lceil(3m+h)/2\rceil$ gives $e-m-1\le m-1$;
all triples read from deficit centres $m+3,\ldots,e$ are
therefore inside the exact identity range. This bound follows
also for $A$: the subtracted-source density relative to
$C^\infty$ is nondecreasing, so it cannot move a first mode
to the right of the infinite first mode. It is not inferred
merely from coefficientwise domination.

At every such centre the preceding/current actual clipped
deficits equal their signed values. If the last next coefficient
is clipped, the actual NEXT deficit is smaller than its raw signed
value, which improves the minor. Thus $b$ is LC at all required
centres. Starting at $A_e^2\ge A_{e-1}A_{e+1}$, the registered
backward geometric-deficit identity gives LC of $A$ down through
centre $m+2$. If $e\le m+1$ no such integration is needed.
The independently proved strict ACTUAL seeds at centres $m,m+1$
complete the entrance. Those seeds include the exact signed
Cassini amplitude, first defect one, second defect $W+3$, and
positive-part boundary transport.

Apply the fixed-padding count induction of the sharpened $h+1$
principle: its proof never changes the padding value, and therefore
uses this one entrance at padding two only. Its two-term positive-
slope propagation yields all moving-centre LC comparisons for
every $u\ge h+1$. The complete homogeneous reference C theorem
from $W+1$, the first-defect improvement, TWO ordinary F seeds
and the full finite D/F/G integration then pay every shift at all
$N\ge W+1$. This is precisely the previously proved joining
argument, with its formerly open entrance now supplied at padding two.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Change to the remaining finite entrance.}
The preceding large-padding theorem already proves all
$\alpha\ge h+1$ at $u=h+1$. This theorem removes $\alpha=2$
from the residual set. For arbitrary CI degree the outstanding
single-entrance paddings are now $\alpha=1$ and $3\le\alpha\le h$.
No assertion of general C, A2 or original unimodality follows
without those and the other recorded unresolved branches.
