\paragraph{A ternary diagonal with two negative atoms.}
For $U_j=[z^j](1+z)^u/(1-z)^3$ put
$Q_{u,j}=U_{j+1}-2\phi U_j+\phi^2U_{j-1}$.
At $u=3j$, the Lagrange diagonal is $[t^{j+1}]G(w)$, where
$w=t(1+w)^3$ and
\[
 G(z)=\frac{(1-\phi z)^2}{(1-z)(1-z^2)^2(1-2z)}.
\]
For the base $H=(1-\phi z)^2/((1-z)(1-2z))$,
$H_0=1,H_1=3-2\phi$ and
$H_n=2^{n-1}(5-3\phi)-(2-\phi)$ for $n\ge2$.
Only $H_1,H_2$ are negative. After multiplication by $(1-z^2)^{-2}$,
only $G_1=3-2\phi$ and $G_3=24-15\phi$ are negative:
$G_2=10-5\phi$, $G_4=57-33\phi$.
Even coefficients have the positive block
$(a+1)+a(8-5\phi)>0$; odd coefficients from rank five have
$a(99-60\phi)-75+45\phi>0$ for $a\ge2$.

Coefficientwise, $w^l-t^l\preceq3l w^{l+1}$, since the difference
is $t^l((3lw-1)(1+w)^{3l}+1)$, a polynomial with nonnegative
coefficients in $w$ after its constant and linear cancellations.
Pair $G_1w$ with $G_2w^2$, and $G_3w^3$ with $G_4w^4$.
Their remaining coefficients are
$A=19-11\phi\ge9/8$ and $B=273-168\phi\ge0$.
For $k\ge4$, $[t^k]w^2\ge6$, so the diagonal exceeds $6A>3$.
For $k=3$, its lower bound is $6A+G_3=138-81\phi>3$.
Thus $Q_{3j,j}>3$ for ALL $j\ge2$.
Pascal recurrence $Q_{u+1,j}=Q_{u,j}+Q_{u,j-1}$ extends this to
ALL $u\ge3j$ by induction in $j$ and then $u$; the bottom row
$Q_{u,1}=\binom u2+3u+7-\phi(2u+5)$ is positive for $u\ge6$.
One must NOT multiply a signed diagonal by $(1+w)^e$ and discard
its negative terms. The Pascal induction is the valid extension.

$Q_s\ge\binom{u-1}{m+1}$.

\[
 \binom{3m-1}{m+1}\ge5(m+2)^2 2^{\lceil m/2\rceil}.
\]
