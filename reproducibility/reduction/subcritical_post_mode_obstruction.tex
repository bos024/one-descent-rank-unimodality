\[
 X=(1,2,\ldots,\kappa-1),\quad c=\kappa(\kappa-1)/2,
 \quad g=L+14,\quad t=\kappa+L+14,\quad

\]

deficit $e$ of degree $D=c+1-\kappa$, and full mixed

$g\le\kappa-2$. The opposite weight is $b=c+L+14$.

\[

\[
