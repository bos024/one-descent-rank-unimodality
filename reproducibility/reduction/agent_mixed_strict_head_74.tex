\begin{theorem}[Absolute first-source threshold 74 for positive mixed recovery]
Assume $F_Z(j+1)\le F_Z(j)$ and that the ENTIRE opposite SOURCE

then $T\le H$, with no OLD A2 premise.  With the usual OLD

The previous 383 minimum-degree and 239 full-staircase suffix proofs
remain independently valid weaker certificates.  The present proof

it does not need the right-heavy CI primitive conclusion beyond its
exact support formula.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{The positive window and the raw source.}
The upper-center reflection of the actual old ordinary product gives
$k<j$ from $p_n>p_k$.  The SECOND strict window gives the stronger
\[
 (k+1)+(n+1)<a+b,\qquad k\le j-3.                    \tag{1}
\]
Indeed if the displayed sum were at least $a+b$, every actual
upper-centered interval-product layer would give
$p_{k+1}\ge p_{n+1}$, a contradiction.
Positive recovery has $r\ge-1$, so $j\le a+1$ and (1) gives
$k+1\le a-1$.  Thus every used head coefficient of the RAW owner

\[

is nonnegative: it agrees with the established positive corrected

\paragraph{A sharper universal head bound from the first source birth.}
The previous mixed identities give $e_n\ge d_r$ and $T\le d_{r+1}-d_r$

\[

\]

coefficient is $q_Z(r+K-1)$; under that inequality its reflected

$q_Z(r+K-1)\ge q_Z(r+1)\ge d_{r+1}-d_r$.
The birth is present because its ordinary index is then positive.
Consequently positive recovery NECESSARILY satisfies
\[
                 e_*\le d_*-3,\qquad
                         2r\ge D-d_*+1.              \tag{2}
\]
Combine (2), $h\le D$, $a=\alpha+D+h$, and the previously proved
linear owner bound $D\le12d_*-25$:
\[
 \begin{split}
 2j=2a-2r
 &\le2\alpha+3D+d_*-1\\
 &\le2\alpha+37d_*-76
   =37K-35\alpha-76\le37K-111.
 \end{split}                                           \tag{3}
\]
For $r=-1$, the first source birth would pay its unit recovery

\[
            k+1\le N:=\left\lfloor\frac{37K-111}{2}\right\rfloor.

\]
The arithmetic in (2)--(4) is independent of both arm counts and
all capacities.

\paragraph{The opposite arm is STRICTLY increasing throughout this head.}
Its main box is $L_Y=[\beta+1]\prod_{v\in V}[v]$, of degree

\[
 M\ge M_{\min}:=1+\frac{(K-2)(K-1)}2,\qquad

\]
For the second inequality, if the largest interval is a capacity
interval, remove it and sum the forced remaining degrees
$1,\ldots,K-3$, together with the positive initial run.
If the initial interval of degree $\beta$ is largest, removing it
leaves all forced non-one degrees $1,\ldots,K-2$, an even stronger bound.

$K^2-77K+226=(K-74)(K-3)+4\ge0$; the second from

The CI support formula therefore gives
$\Delta(L_Y)_s\ge1$ for every $0\le s\le N$.

 q_Y=L_Y+\sum_{i=1}^{g}z^{w_i}\prod_{l>i}[V_l],

\paragraph{A SINGLE nonnegative convolution term pays the absolute branch.}

\[
            H=[z^{k+1}]\{R_Z\,(1-z)q_Y\}.            \tag{8}
\]
All summands used in (8) are nonnegative by (1), (4), and (7).

\[
 \begin{split}

      \ge R_Z(r+1)\\
  &=q_Z(r+1)+d_{r+1}\ge d_{r+1}-d_r\ge T.

For $r\ge k$, the already proved DIRECT raw-kernel LC relative
