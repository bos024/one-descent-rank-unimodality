\section{A coefficientwise lower source profile determined by first multiplicity}
Let $[t]=1+\cdots+z^{t-1}$, with $[0]=0$, and define
\[
 M_0=0,\qquad M_{d+1}=1+[d]M_d\quad(d\ge0).
\]
Thus $M_1=1$, $M_2=2$, $M_3=3+2z$,
$M_4=4+5z+5z^2+2z^3$.

\begin{theorem}[Normalized first-source multiplicity profile]
Let $X$ be an actual increasing arm, $\kappa$ its first missing
weight, $\delta=E_X(\kappa)$, and $A=z^{-\kappa}E_X$.
Then, coefficientwise,
\[
                                A\succeq M_\delta.      \tag{1}
\]
The same theorem holds for the larger formal append grammar with
nondecreasing first missing weights. Equality is attained, for
every $\delta\ge2$, by the staircase host $(1,2,\ldots,\delta-1)$.
The assertion concerns the normalized entire SOURCE polynomial,
not coefficientwise dominance of its differences.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For an initial all-ones arm, $\kappa=2$, and its first source
coefficient is either one or two. Nonnegativity supplies $M_1$
or $M_2$ directly. Retain the established bound $\delta\le\kappa$.
Under a legal append $t\ge\kappa-1$, the exact source recurrence is
$E'=z[t]E+z^{t+1}$. There are three exhaustive normalized cases.

If $t>\kappa$, then $\kappa'=\kappa+1$, $\delta'=\delta$, and
\[
                         A'=[t]A+z^{t-\kappa}\succeq A.
\]
Thus (1) is preserved. If $t=\kappa$, the two first sources tie:
$\kappa'=\kappa+1$, $\delta'=\delta+1$, and
\[
 A'=1+[\kappa]A\succeq1+[\delta]M_\delta=M_{\delta+1}.
\]
Here $\delta\le\kappa$ is exactly the needed capacity budget.
Finally, if $t=\kappa-1$, the first missing weight stays fixed
and the new first source is the single birth:
\[
                 \delta'=1,\qquad A'=1+z[t]A\succeq M_1.
\]
These cases prove the unbounded induction. Sorted actual arms
satisfy $t\ge\kappa-1$, since every old first missing weight is
at most the old maximum plus one.

For the staircase, the initial $(1)$ has $\kappa=\delta=2$
and normalized source $2=M_2$. Every next capacity equals the
old first weight; the tied-source equality applies at every step.
This proves sharpness for all $\delta\ge2$.
\end{proof}

In particular, for $\delta\ge2$,
\[
 E_X(\kappa+1)\ge\binom\delta2-1,\qquad
 E_X(\kappa)+E_X(\kappa+1)\ge\binom{\delta+1}{2}-1.
\]
The first coefficient formula follows from
$[z]M_{d+1}=[z]M_d+d$ for $d\ge2$, starting from $[z]M_2=0$.
Also $F_X(1)=E_X(1)\ge M_\delta(1)$, where
$M_{d+1}(1)=1+dM_d(1)$.
These are complete coefficient/mass consequences of (1).
