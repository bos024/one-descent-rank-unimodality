\section{Complete actual C for every pure ternary block}
Write $[t]=1+\cdots+z^{t-1}$ and $S=z[3]$.
For the actual increasing arm $X=1^\alpha3^r$, put
\[
 F_X=\sum_{j<r}S^j+S^r[\alpha+1],\quad
 D_N=(F_N-F_{N-1})^+\ (N\ge2),\quad D_0=D_1=0,
 \qquad G_k=\sum_{i=0}^kF_i.
\]
We use zero extension at negative indices. Property C is
\[
 G_kD_{N-1}\ge G_{k-1}D_N
       \quad(N\ge4,\ 1\le k\le N-3).                 \tag{C}
\]
These are exactly all shifts at least three. The cases $k\le0$
are automatic. This section proves (C) for EVERY $\alpha\ge1$
and $r\ge0$. It does not prove general C or original unimodality.

\paragraph{The analytic large-block range.}
Assume first $r\ge18$. Let $H=(1-S)^{-1}$, with coefficients
$h_n$, and let $R_N=h_N-h_{N-1}$ for $N\ge2$. Let
$\lambda^3=\lambda^2+\lambda+1$, $1<\lambda<2$, and set
$a=\lambda-1$, $b=1/\lambda$, $B=1+az+bz^2$.
The frozen proper-quotient and full-primitive theorems give
\[
 E=[3]^r=BJ+R_0+R_1z,                              \tag{1}
\]
where J is positive, globally LC, and has globally LC full positive
primitive. The pure norm is $b^{r+1}\le1/64$ for $r\ge18$,
so this is in the proved norm cone. Write M for the FIRST mode of J,
and $x_s=\sum_{j\ge1}b^jJ_{s+j}$, $Y_s=x_s+x_{s-1}$.
Ordinary LC convolution gives global LC of Y, and
\[
                \lambda Y_{s-1}-Y_s=J_s+J_{s-1}\ge0. \tag{2}
\]
In particular $Y_s\le\lambda Y_{s-1}<2Y_{s-1}$ whenever
the predecessor is positive.

\paragraph{Exact identification, including the low-degree remainder.}
Put $d^\infty=(1+z)\sum_{j<r}S^j$. Directly from the displayed
actual F one obtains the exact signed/clipped identity
\[
 d_n=D_{n+2}
   =\bigl(d^\infty_n-(z^{\alpha+r-1}E)_n\bigr)^+.
                                                               \tag{3}
\]
The whole residual is
\[
 \lambda d^\infty_{n-1}-d^\infty_n
 =[z^n]\frac{(1+z)(z^rE-1)}{B}
 =[z^n]\{z^r(1+z)J+C\},\qquad \deg C\le r.       \tag{4}
\]
Here $C=(1+z)(z^r(R_0+R_1z)-1)/B$ is a polynomial: at the
two roots of B, $S=1$ and therefore $z^rE=S^r=1$.
The roots are distinct; equivalently this divisibility follows by
reducing powers using $S\equiv1\pmod B$.
The future-geometric solution of (4), with finite terminal support,
gives exactly
\[
                  d^\infty_{r+s}=Y_s\quad(s\ge0).     \tag{5}
\]
Equation (5) is NOT asserted at $s=-1$. With $c=\alpha-1$,
(3) becomes $(Y_s-E_{s-c})^+$ for $s\ge0$.

Let L be the first mode of the actual D. The registered actual
finite-padding density $D/d^\infty$ is nonincreasing, hence
the actual shifted first mode is no later than that of $d^\infty$.
The sequence $x_s$ is nonincreasing from $s=M-1$, so Y is
nonincreasing from $s=M$. Using the already proved unimodality of
$d^\infty$ and (5) gives
\[
                         L-2\le r+M.                \tag{6}
\]
Thus every strict pre-L centre $N=r+2+s$ has $s<M$.
The new all-order cut theorem at $t=3$ proves actual D LC for
all such centres with $s\ge c+3$. Its degree-one remainder
is absent on every one of the three source reads. This is the
precise place (1), (5), and the first-mode guard are used.

\paragraph{All three startup centres and the first physical centre.}
The source coefficients are
\[
 E_0=1,\quad E_1=r,\quad E_2=r(r+1)/2,
 \quad E_3=r(r-1)(r+4)/6.
\]
Before the source starts the Y triple is LC. At centre $c-1$
only the next value is lowered, improving its minor. At centres
$c,c+1,c+2$ with $s\ge1$, expand the new minor about
$Y=(P,T,U)$ and use $T\le2P$. Respectively the lower bounds are
\[
\begin{split}
 M_Y+(r-4)P+1,&\\
 M_Y+(E_2-4r)P+U+r^2-E_2,&\\
 M_Y+(E_3-4E_2)P+rU+E_2^2-rE_3.&
\end{split}                                                   \tag{7}
\]
Every term is nonnegative for $r\ge18$; in particular
$E_2^2-rE_3=r^2(r^2+11)/12$. At a strict pre-L centre all actual
three values are positive, so the signed formulas in (7) are valid.
A zero neighbour makes the LC assertion automatic. No previous
Y value outside the exact range (5) has been substituted.

The omitted physical centre is $A=r+2$, where the previous
actual value is still $R_{A-1}$. The exact first two losses are
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &\alpha=1&\alpha=2&\alpha\ge3\\ \hline
 R_A-D_A&2&1&1\\
 R_{A+1}-D_{A+1}&2r+2&r+3&r+2 .
 \end{array}                                                   \tag{8}
\]
For a strict pre-L centre these are signed losses, without clipping.
The frozen tribonacci minor bound gives
$R_A^2-R_{A-1}R_{A+1}\ge-R_{A-1}$, and
$R_A\le2R_{A-1}$. For losses $v,w$ in (8), the new minor is
at least
\[
                    (w-4v-1)R_{A-1}+v^2\ge0.       \tag{9}
\]
Combining (6)--(9) proves
\[
       D_N^2\ge D_{N-1}D_{N+1}\qquad(A\le N<L).     \tag{10}
\]
Only strict pre-first-mode LC is asserted. Post-mode actual D LC
is not needed and is not claimed.

\paragraph{The actual ordinary entry seed.}
Put $T_n=h_n-\lambda h_{n-1}$, with $h_{-1}=0$.
Its generating function is $1/B$, so $T_0=1,T_1=-a$ and
$T_{n+2}=-bT_n-aT_{n+1}$. The exact invariant is
\[
 bT_{n-1}^2+aT_{n-1}T_n+T_n^2=b^n\quad(n\ge1).
\]
The registered coercivity bound for this quadratic form gives
$T_n^2\le4b^n<1$ for $n\ge3$, since $b<3/5$.
Consequently $|\lambda R_A-R_{A+1}|=|T_A-T_{A+1}|<2$.
Equations (8) and $\lambda<2$ imply
\[
             \lambda D_A-D_{A+1}\ge r-4>0.         \tag{11}
\]
In the only nontrivial case $D_{A+1}>D_A>0$, set
$\mu_1=D_{A+1}/D_A$. Since
$D_A\le R_A=h_r+h_{r-1}\le2h_r$, (11) gives
\[
 h_{r+1}-\mu h_r
   =T_{r+1}+(\lambda-\mu)h_r
   \ge-1+(r-4)/2\ge6>0\quad\text{for every }\mu\le\mu_1.
                                                               \tag{12}
\]
This is the ordinary F entry at index $A-1$. It is not inferred
from cumulative LC of the actual sequence.

\paragraph{All ranks and all shifts of C.}
Through $A-1$ the actual F and D coincide with H and its derivative.
The registered renewal cumulative is globally LC, its adjacent
ratios tend to lambda and are therefore at least lambda, and H

D is lowered and C still holds.

For $A<N\le L$, discard the automatic case $D_N\le D_{N-1}$.
Otherwise let $\mu=D_N/D_{N-1}>1$. By (10),
$\mu\le\mu_1<\lambda$ and $D_j-\mu D_{j-1}\ge0$
for every $A\le j\le N$. The true seed (12) and
\[
 (F_j-\mu F_{j-1})-(F_{j-1}-\mu F_{j-2})
                         =D_j-\mu D_{j-1}
\]
make every later F residual nonnegative through N. The protected
G residual at $A-1$ is nonnegative by its reference ratio at least
lambda. One more cumulative summation gives
$G_k-\mu G_{k-1}\ge0$ for all $A\le k\le N-3$;
the earlier $k$ were already paid by the protected prefix.
Multiplication by $D_{N-1}$ proves (C). For $N>L$, unimodality
of the actual D gives $D_N\le D_{N-1}$, and increasing G pays C
directly, including terminal zero entries. This completes the
unbounded $r\ge18$, arbitrary-alpha proof.

\paragraph{The complete small-block entrance.}

\[
 11\le r\le17,\quad 1\le\alpha\le r,\quad
 4\le N\le2r,\quad1\le k\le N-3.                 \tag{13}

There are exactly 98 hosts. ALL literal integer products in (13)
