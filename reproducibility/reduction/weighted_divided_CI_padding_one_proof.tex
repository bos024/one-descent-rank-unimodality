\section{A weighted divided-CI primitive prefix for padding one}
The following all-degree theorem has an unbounded written induction,
independent exact QA of its heads and junctions, and six scoped Lean
scalar certificates. Its actual consequence is stated separately.
General C, A2 and original UI are not asserted here.

For $L=\prod[t_i]$, $t_i\ge3$, degree $h$, and $u\ge h+1$, put
\[
 E=(2+z)(1+z)^uL,\quad c=q^2\psi^uL(-\phi),\quad
 J=(E-c)/(1+qz),\quad K=u-2.
\]
The theorem states that $J$ is positive ordinary LC and
its positive primitive is LC at EVERY centre $1\le n\le K$.
No assertion of full primitive LC beyond this prefix is needed.

\paragraph{The finite-prefix window extension.}
The proved nonsymmetric window theorem has the following immediate
prefix version. If $a$ is globally LC and its positive primitive
is LC through centre $K$, then $[t]a$ has the same two properties
through centre $K$, for EVERY $t\ge1$.
Let $m$ be the first mode of $a$. If $m\le K$, its ENTIRE positive
primitive is LC, since it is zero past $m$, so the existing theorem
applies. If $m\ge K+1$, all differences read at ranks at most
$K+1$ are still nonnegative; causal convolution of the LC primitive
prefix proves all required minors through $K$. These two cases
include $m=K$ and $m=K+1$ without a missing joining centre.

\paragraph{Global ordinary LC of the weighted quotient.}
Let $J_0=((1+z)^uL-\psi^uL(-\phi))/(1+qz)$.
It is positive global LC by the registered whole-deficit theorem
($m'=u-1\ge h$). The exact identity
\[
 J=(2+z)J_0+\phi\psi^uL(-\phi)
\]
changes ONLY the constant coefficient of a global-LC convolution.
All ordinary minors from centre two onward are unchanged.
For nonempty $L$ put $r=\operatorname{len}L$, $v=u+r\ge4$.
Then $3r\le v-1$, $E_0=2$, $E_1=2v+1$, $E_2=v^2+2r$, and
$|c|\le(4/5)q^3<1/5$.
At the first centre, with $w=2v+1$, the exact ordinary minor is
\[
                   w^2-(2-c)(E_2+qw).
\]
Using $q\le2/3$, $-1/5\le c\le1/5$, and $3r\le v-1$, this
is at least $(9v^2-2v+5)/5>0$. Its constant coefficient
$2-c$ is positive, and every later coefficient is an unchanged
positive convolution value. For empty $L$ the same calculation
works for $u\ge2$ with $r=0$, while $u=1$ is explicit below.

\paragraph{The weighted binary base, all $u$.}
For $L=1$ the divided quotient is denoted $T_u$. It satisfies
\[
 T_{u+1}=(1+z)T_u+\phi c_u,\qquad c_u=q^2\psi^u.
\]
Only primitive ranks zero/one change. The weighted-kernel closure
therefore proves all primitive centres at least three by induction.
At $u=1$ the quotient is $(2\phi-1)+\phi z$, whose positive
primitive has a single entry. At $u=2,3$ the rank-two primitive
is nonpositive; at $u=4,5$ the rank-three primitive is nonpositive.
These base signs follow by exact division in $\mathbb Q(\phi)$.
For $u=2,3$, the rank-two differences are $1-2\phi,3-2\phi$.
For $u=4,5$, the rank-three differences are $4-5\phi,9-6\phi$.

For $u\ge2$, $|c_u|\le q^4<1/6$. The exact first primitive
minor is
\[
 2u^2+4u+1-4\phi u+2\phi
       +c_u\{u^2+\phi(2u-3)\}+\phi c_u^2
 \ge(11u^2-28u+36)/6\ge4.
\]
At centre two write its minor as $M_{20}+c_u A_u$, where
\[
\begin{aligned}
 M_{20}&=u^4/3+(2-4\phi)u^3/3+13u^2/6
                         +(-25/6+13\phi/3)u+\phi-2,\\
 A_u&=1+2u-u^2+\phi(-u/6+3u^2/2-u^3/3).
\end{aligned}
\]
For $u\ge6$, $-2u^3/3\le A_u\le0$ and the coefficient of
$\phi$ in $M_{20}$ is negative. Thus $\phi\le2$ and $c_u\le1/6$
give
\[
 M_2\ge\frac{u(6u^3-38u^2+39u+81)}{18}>0.
\]
At $u=6+w$ the cubic is $6w^3+70w^2+231w+243$.
This proves the entire base primitive LC, not just a finite check.

\paragraph{Adding an arbitrary interval, keeping the same prefix.}
Induct on the number of tail factors with fixed $u$.
For $L'=L_0[t]$ one has $u\ge\deg L_0+t$, and the divided
quotient correction to $[t]J_{L_0}$ again has degree $t-2$.
The prefix window lemma inherits every centre $t+1\le n\le K$.
For lower centres set
\[
 B=(2+z)L_0Q_u,\quad a=\phi^2c,\quad X_n=B_n-a\psi^n.
\]
Here $|a|\le(4/5)q<1/2$. Normalizing the numerator by two,
its first coefficient is $r+1/2\le(u-1)/2$, where
$r=\operatorname{len}L_0$ and $2r\le\deg L_0\le u-t$.
The registered robust coefficient budget scales to
$M_B(n)\ge2q^nT_B(n)$, so $X$ is positive and LC throughout
the read head $0\le n\le u-1$, with LC centres $1,\ldots,u-2$.
For $1\le n<t$, the actual primitive equals $X_n$; at $t,t+1$
it equals $X_t-2$ and $X_{t+1}-B_1$. These identities are used
only inside the head. If $t>K$ the corresponding later boundary
is not read. Centre one is treated separately below.

Centres $2\le n\le\min(t-2,K)$ are paid by $X$ LC. At centre
$t-1\le K$, lowering the next value by two improves the minor.
At the remaining centre $t\le K$, its exact raw minor is
\[
                 M_X(t)-4X_t+4+B_1X_{t-1}.
\]
The normalized coefficient identity gives
\[
 B_0=2,\quad B_1=2(u+r-q)+1,\quad
 B_1^2-4B_2=4(u-r-q^2)+1=:D.
\]
Since $2r\le u-t\le u-3$,
$D\ge2u+5$, $B_{t-1}\ge2(u-q)\ge2(u-1)$, and $B_1\le3u$.
Global LC of $B$ gives $B_1B_t\le B_2B_{t-1}$, whence
$B_1B_{t-1}-4B_t\ge4u/3$.
The alternating correction is at most
\[
 |a|q^{t-1}(B_1+4q)\le(1/5)(B_1+4q)\le3u/5.
\]
Therefore $B_1X_{t-1}-4X_t\ge11u/15$, and the junction
minor is at least $4+11u/15$.

\paragraph{Correctly normalized first primitive minor.}
For nonempty $L'$ put $r'=\operatorname{len}L'$, $v=u+r'\ge4$,
$w=2v+1$, $E_2=v^2+2r'$. With $|c|<1/5$ its actual minor is
\[
 w^2-2E_2-2\phi(w-2)
       +c\{E_2+\phi(w-4)\}+\phi c^2.
\]
It is at least $(9v^2-24v+31-22r')/5$, hence at least $57/5$
by $3r'\le v-1$, $v\ge4$.
Global ordinary LC of the actual $J$ makes its positive-primitive
support an initial interval, so every clipped/zero case is covered.
This completes the prefix induction at every CI degree and every
$u\ge h+1$. The independent QA checks all displayed identities
without floating-point comparisons. \texttt{WeightedDividedCI.lean}
certifies the first ordinary head, both arbitrary-real binary heads,
the nonempty first primitive head, and both last-junction payments.
The prefix-window and polynomial induction assembly are written;
this is not an end-to-end Lean formalization of actual arms.

\paragraph{Actual padding-one consequence.}
At the single entrance $m=h$, $u=m+1$, the actual raw geometric
deficit equals $[z^s](1-z)J$ for $1\le s\le u$.
Only primitive centres $2\le s\le u-2$ are used in backward
coefficient integration; the two old actual strict coefficient
seeds pay the exceptional initial centres. The same first-mode
and fixed-padding count/read join as padding two prove
all late actual C at padding one and binary counts at least $h+1$.
The complete actual argument is in
\texttt{actual\_padding\_one\_CI\_entrance.tex}. This removes padding
one from the single-entrance residual, leaving $3\le\alpha\le h$;
it does not remove the other unresolved general-C/A2 branches.
