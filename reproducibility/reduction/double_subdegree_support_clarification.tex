\section{Explicit support convention for the new robust-head statement}
Read the phrase ``nonnegative LC numerator'' in
\texttt{double\_subdegree\_CI\_entrance\_proof.tex} with the
INTERVAL-SUPPORT hypothesis of the registered Toeplitz convolution
theorem: there are NO internal zeros in its positive support.
Equivalently, the numerator belongs to the nonnegative TP2 sequence
class. This hypothesis is used in the Cauchy--Binet step and in
$N_{s+1}\le N_1N_s$. Merely checking adjacent three-term
inequalities while allowing a long internal gap is insufficient.

Every numerator actually used for the quotient, actual derivative
tail and late-C application is a product of interval polynomials.
It has positive coefficients throughout its support. Thus the new
all-padding actual late-C theorem, its finite exception coverage,
and its Lean kernels are unchanged. This note makes the GENERIC
robust-head statement's support convention explicit; it does not
extend that statement to gapped nonnegative sequences.
